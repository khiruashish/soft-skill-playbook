    On the Flight & Arrival¶

    Today's goal: talk to cabin crew and passengers, handle seat problems and connections, and get through immigration, baggage and customs on arrival. (See Airport for check-in and security.)

    Key words¶

    Word / phrase Meaning
    cabin crew / flight attendant staff on the plane
    overhead bin / locker storage above seats
    aisle / window / middle seat seat positions
    seat belt sign light that says: stay seated, belt on
    turbulence bumpy air
    connecting flight / connection your next flight
    transit / transfer desk help desk for connections
    landing card / arrival form form for immigration
    baggage claim / carousel / belt where bags arrive
    customs / declare check of goods / tell officials what you're carrying

    Questions you'll ask (on board)¶

    • Excuse me, I think you're in my seat. I'm in 14C.
    • Could you help me put my bag in the overhead bin?
    • Is it possible to change seats? There's an empty one in row 20.
    • Could I have some water / a blanket / a vegetarian meal?
    • What time do we land, local time?
    • Will I make my connection? I only have 50 minutes.
    • Which gate does my connecting flight leave from?
    • Do I need to collect my bag before my connecting flight?

    Questions you'll be asked¶

    Crew / officer asks You answer
    Chicken or vegetarian? Vegetarian, please.
    Something to drink? Just water, please. / Tea with milk, no sugar.
    Could you put your seat upright / open the window shade? Sure, sorry.
    Purpose of your visit? (immigration) Business. I'm attending a meeting at our office here.
    How long are you staying? Six days. I fly back on the 18th.
    Anything to declare? (customs) No, nothing. / Yes, I have some sweets for my family.

    Conversation 1: on board¶

    YouExcuse me, I think you're sitting in my seat. I'm in 22A, the window.
    PassengerOh, sorry! I thought this was 21. Let me move.
    YouNo problem. Thank you.
    CrewGood evening. Could you put your bag under the seat in front, please? The bins are full.
    YouSure. Could I have a blanket, please? It's a bit cold.
    CrewOf course, I'll bring one.
    YouThanks. And do you know what time we land in Singapore?
    CrewAt 6:40 local time. That's about 20 minutes early.
    YouGreat. I have a connecting flight to Sydney. Do I need to collect my bag?
    CrewIf it's on the same ticket, no. It's checked through. The transfer desk can confirm.
    YouThank you very much.

    Conversation 2: immigration on arrival¶

    OfficerGood morning. Passport, please.
    YouGood morning. Here you are.
    OfficerWhat's the purpose of your visit?
    YouBusiness. I'm attending a training at our Sydney office.
    OfficerHow long will you stay?
    YouSeven days. My return flight is on the 22nd.
    OfficerWhere are you staying?
    YouAt the Harbour View Hotel. Here's the booking.
    OfficerIs this your first visit to Australia?
    YouYes, it is.
    OfficerAre you carrying any food?
    YouYes, some packaged snacks. I've declared them on the form.
    OfficerOkay. Enjoy your stay.
    YouThank you.

    When things go wrong¶

    • "My flight was delayed and I've missed my connection. Can you rebook me?"
    • "My bag hasn't arrived. Here's my baggage tag. Where do I report it?"
    • "My bag is damaged: the handle is broken. I'd like to file a report."
    • "I feel unwell. Could I have some water? Do you have anything for nausea?"
    • "Sorry, I didn't understand the question. Could you repeat it, please?"

    Practice¶

    Answer the immigration officer in one clear sentence each: purpose, length of stay, where you're staying, your job.

    Sample answers

    I'm here on holiday. · For ten days. · At my sister's house in Melbourne; here's the address. · I'm a software engineer.

    Speak today¶

    • Practise the immigration answers out loud until you can say them without stopping
    • Learn 3 on-board requests by heart: water, blanket, help with a bag
