    Everyday Life¶

    Talking at the airport or on a train builds the same confidence you use at work. Each page lists the questions to ask, the questions you'll be asked, ready-made phrases, and what to do when things go wrong.

    • Airport

      Check-in, security, immigration, boarding, delays, lost bags.

    • Train

      Tickets, platforms, seats, ticket examiner, missed trains.

      Open

    • Hotel

      Check-in, room issues, requests, check-out.

    • Restaurant & Café

      Ordering, dietary needs, complaints, the bill.

    • Cab & Directions

      Booking, talking to drivers, asking strangers the way.

    • Doctor & Pharmacy

      Describing symptoms, asking about medicines.

    • Bank & Shopping

      Start, keep going, and exit any conversation.

    The universal formula for strangers

    Greeting + polite opener + specific question + thanks. "Good morning. Excuse me, could you tell me where Gate 22 is? Thank you so much."
