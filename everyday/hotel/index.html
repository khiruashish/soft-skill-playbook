    Phase 3 · Everyday Life · Day 27 of 84

    At the Hotel¶

    Polite + specific = fast service. Front-desk staff solve problems all day. Make yours easy to solve.

    Check-in¶

    They ask You say
    "Do you have a reservation?" "Yes, under the name Ashish Gupta, for 3 nights."
    "May I see your ID and a card for incidentals?" "Sure. Is the deposit refundable at check-out?"
    "Would you like breakfast included?" "How much extra is it per day?"

    You can ask:

    • "Is early check-in possible? My flight landed early."
    • "What time is check-out? Is late check-out available?"
    • "What's the Wi-Fi password?"
    • "What time is breakfast, and where?"
    • "Could I have a quiet room / high floor / away from the lift?"
    • "Is there an airport shuttle? What time does it leave?"
    • "Do you have a gym / laundry service / business centre?"
    • "Can I leave my luggage here after check-out?"

    Problems with the room¶

    "Hi, this is room 512. The AC isn't cooling, and the room is quite warm. Could you send someone to check it, or move me to another room? Thank you."

    Other requests:

    • "Could I get extra towels / pillows, please?"
    • "The hot water isn't working."
    • "There's a lot of noise from the next room. Could you help?"
    • "I booked a non-smoking room, but this one smells of smoke."

    Check-out¶

    • "I'd like to check out. Room 512."
    • "Could I see an itemised bill? I don't think I used the minibar."
    • "Could you send the invoice to my email with my company's name and GST number?"
    • "Could you call a cab to the airport, please?"

    Practice conversations¶

    Checking in early¶

    ReceptionistGood morning, welcome to the Residency. How can I help you?
    YouGood morning. I have a reservation under Ashish Gupta for three nights.
    ReceptionistYes, I have it. Check-in is at 2 pm, though.
    YouMy flight landed early. Is early check-in possible?
    ReceptionistLet me check… A room will be ready at 11. Would you like to leave your bags here?
    YouYes, please. Is breakfast still available?
    ReceptionistYes, until 10:30 in the restaurant on the first floor.
    YouGreat. And what's the Wi-Fi password?
    ReceptionistIt's on this card. Here's your key once the room is ready.

    A problem with the room¶

    YouHi, this is room 512. The AC isn't cooling, and the room is very warm.
    ReceptionistI'm sorry. I'll send a technician right away.
    Twenty minutes later
    YouHi again. The technician says the AC needs a part, and it can't be fixed today.
    ReceptionistI apologise. We can move you to another room on the 6th floor.
    YouThat would be great. Is it also non-smoking?
    ReceptionistYes, it is. Someone will help you with your bags.
    YouThank you. I appreciate the quick help.
