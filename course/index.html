    Course Roadmap¶

    75 days, five phases, about 20–30 minutes a day. Do one lesson a day, in order. Each lesson has a goal, the key language, a real conversation, and a practice task. Tick off each day as you finish it; your browser remembers your progress.

    Your progress: 0 days done

    How each day works¶

    1. Learn — read the lesson: key words, patterns and example sentences (10 min)
    2. Listen & read aloud — read each conversation out loud, playing both roles (5 min)
    3. Practise — do the exercises and check the answers (5 min)
    4. Role-play — run the day's lesson with the course master prompt (5–10 min)
    5. Use it — use at least one new sentence in real life today

    Start with the master prompt

    Copy the course tutor prompt from the Master Prompt page into any AI assistant, then just type Start Day 1. It will teach, role-play, quiz and correct you.

    Phases¶

    • Phase 1 · English Basics

      Days 1–13. Build sentences, ask and answer any question, and use the tenses and polite forms you need every day.

      Open phase

    • Phase 2 · Communication & Confidence

      Days 14–18. The principles behind every good conversation, and the confidence to start one.

      Open phase

    • Phase 3 · Everyday Conversations

      Days 19–48. Real conversations for travel, food, shopping, health, fitness, home, offices and social life.

      Open phase

    • Phase 4 · Workplace Communication

      Days 49–64. Stand-ups, managers, juniors, CEOs, clients and every hard conversation in between.

      Open phase

    • Phase 5 · Career Moments

      Days 65–75. The conversations that shape your career, from the interview to your next big move.

      Open phase

    All days¶

    Phase 1 · English Basics¶

    Day Lesson What you'll learn Done
    1 Greetings & Introducing Yourself Hello, goodbye, and a 30-second introduction about you
    2 Building Sentences Word order, am/is/are, and turning ideas into clear sentences
    3 Yes/No Questions Make questions with be, do/does, can, have, and answer them
    4 Wh- Questions What, where, when, why, who, which, how: forming any question
    5 Answering Questions Short answers, full answers, polite 'no', and 'I'm not sure'
    6 Present: Routines & Right Now Daily routine, what's happening now, and the questions for each
    7 Past: What Happened Talk about yesterday, last week, and your experience
    8 Future: Plans & Promises Will, going to, and plans with the present continuous
    9 Polite Requests & Offers Can, could, would, may: request, offer, permission, refusal
    10 Joining Ideas & Opinions And, but, because, so, if, when; agree, disagree, give opinions
    11 Numbers, Time, Dates & Money Prices, phone numbers, times, dates and spelling out loud
    12 Describing People, Places & Problems Adjectives, comparisons, and describing what's wrong
    13 Common Mistakes & Fixes Articles, prepositions, tenses and Indian-English habits

    Phase 2 · Communication & Confidence¶

    Day Lesson What you'll learn Done
    14 Communication Principles Point first, know your audience, PREP, SBAR, STAR
    15 Listening & Asking Questions Active listening and the question types that unblock anything
    16 Building Confidence Habits, body language and voice that make you sound sure
    17 Small Talk & Networking Start, continue and politely end any conversation
    18 Phone Calls Prepare, open, confirm and close a call with confidence
