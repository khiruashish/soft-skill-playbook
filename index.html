    Soft Skill Playbook¶

    Written for software developers: the fresher on day one, and the engineer with 15 years who still dreads the CEO review.

    How to talk to your manager, your juniors and your CEO. How to explain an issue and get through the day-to-day. What to say at the airport, on the train, and in the other places where you have to speak up.

    Start with the Master Prompt Build Confidence

    ListenSpeakExplainAskConfidenceTrust

    Pick your path¶

    • Beginner (0–3 yrs)

      You know the code but freeze in meetings. Start here: the principles, ready-made scripts, and 10-minute daily drills.

      Foundations

    • Mid-level (3–8 yrs)

      You need influence: pushing back on scope, mentoring, explaining trade-offs to people who aren't engineers.

      Workplace

    • Senior / Lead (8+ yrs)

      Executive communication, difficult conversations, and making other people better communicators.

      Talking to the CEO

    • Practise with AI

      A master prompt that turns any AI assistant into a role-play partner and feedback coach.

    What's inside¶

    • Talk to your manager

      1:1s, status updates, asking for help, raising a risk early, negotiating deadlines, asking for a raise.

      Open

    • Talk to juniors

      Teach without talking down to people. Give code-review feedback, delegate, and help a junior grow.

      Open

    • Talk to the CEO

      Bottom line first, then the business impact, then a clear decision for them to make. 30-second and 2-minute versions.

      Open

    • Explain an issue

      The SBAR & STAR frameworks, incident updates, root-cause write-ups, and explaining tech to non-tech people.

      Open

    The one-line philosophy¶

    Soft skills are hard skills you practise out loud.

    Nobody is born good at this. Confident communicators have prepared phrases, have practised them, and reflect on how each conversation went. This playbook gives you all three.
