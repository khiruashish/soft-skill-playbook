## Key words

| Dentist | Eye clinic |
|---|---|
| toothache / sensitivity / cavity | blurry / blurred vision, eye strain |
| filling / root canal / extraction | eye test / power / prescription |
| cleaning / scaling | glasses / spectacles / frames / lenses |
| gums (bleeding, swollen) | contact lenses / anti-glare coating |
| wisdom tooth / braces / crown | dry eyes / itching / redness |
| X-ray / numb / anaesthetic | near-sighted (can't see far) / far-sighted |

## Questions you'll ask

=== "Dentist"

    - **Can I get an appointment** for a check-up this week?
    - **Will it hurt?** Will you give me an injection?
    - **Do I need a root canal**, or is a filling enough?
    - **How many sittings** will it take?
    - **How much will it cost?** Is it covered by insurance?
    - **When can I eat** after the treatment?

=== "Eye clinic"

    - **I'd like an eye test.** Do I need an appointment?
    - **Has my power changed?**
    - **Do I need to wear glasses all the time**, or only for reading / screens?
    - **What's the difference** between these lenses?
    - **How long will the glasses take** to be ready?
    - **Are these drops safe** with contact lenses?

## Conversation 1: at the dentist

```dialogue
Dentist: What seems to be the problem?
You: I have pain in a lower back tooth on the right side. It hurts when I drink something cold.
Dentist: How long has it been like this?
You: About two weeks. It's getting worse.
Dentist: Let me take a look… There's a deep cavity. We'll take an X-ray to check.
You: Will I need a root canal?
Dentist: If the X-ray shows the nerve is affected, yes. Otherwise, a filling will do.
You: How many sittings does a root canal take, and how much does it cost?
Dentist: Usually two or three sittings. It's about ₹6,000, plus ₹8,000 for the crown.
You: Okay. Will it hurt?
Dentist: No, we'll make the area numb first. You'll only feel some pressure.
You: Alright, let's do the X-ray.
```

## Conversation 2: eye test and glasses

```dialogue
Optometrist: Please read the smallest line you can see.
You: E, F, P… sorry, the next line is blurry.
Optometrist: Okay. Do you find it hard to see far away, or close up?
You: Far away. Road signs are blurry when I drive at night. And my eyes get tired after work.
Optometrist: Your power has increased slightly, to minus 1.5 in both eyes.
You: Do I need to wear glasses all the time?
Optometrist: For driving and screens, yes. I'd suggest lenses with a blue-light filter.
You: How much do they cost, and when will they be ready?
Optometrist: ₹3,500 for the lenses, plus the frame. They'll be ready in three days.
You: Can I also get a copy of the prescription?
Optometrist: Of course.
```
