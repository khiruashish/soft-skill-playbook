# Gym

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| membership (monthly / quarterly / annual) | the plan you pay for |
| personal trainer (PT) / trainer | coach |
| warm-up / cool-down / stretching | before / after exercise |
| cardio (treadmill, cycle, cross-trainer) | heart exercise |
| weights / dumbbells / barbell / machines | strength equipment |
| sets and reps | "3 sets of 12 reps" |
| form / posture | the correct way to do an exercise |
| spot (someone) | stand by to help with a heavy lift |
| sore / pulled a muscle / sprain | pain after exercise / injury |

## Questions you'll ask

=== "Joining"

    - **What membership plans** do you have? **Is there a joining fee?**
    - **What are the timings?** Is it open on Sundays?
    - **Is a trainer included**, or is that extra?
    - **Can I try one free session** first?
    - **Can I freeze my membership** if I travel?

=== "With the trainer"

    - **I want to lose weight / build strength / improve stamina.** What do you suggest?
    - **I have knee / back pain.** Which exercises should I avoid?
    - **Is my form correct?**
    - **How many sets and reps** should I do?
    - **What should I eat** before and after a workout?

=== "On the gym floor"

    - **Are you using this machine?** / **How many sets do you have left?**
    - **Can I work in with you?** (share the machine between sets)
    - **Could you spot me**, please?
    - **Could you show me how to adjust** this seat?

## Conversation 1: joining the gym

```dialogue
Receptionist: Hi! Welcome to FitZone. How can I help you?
You: Hi. I'd like to join the gym. What plans do you have?
Receptionist: Monthly is ₹2,000, quarterly is ₹5,000, and annual is ₹15,000.
You: Is there a joining fee?
Receptionist: Not this month. We have an offer.
You: Does it include a trainer?
Receptionist: A general trainer is included. A personal trainer is ₹6,000 extra per month.
You: Okay. Can I try one free session before I decide?
Receptionist: Sure, come tomorrow at 7 am. Bring sports shoes and a towel.
You: Great. What are the timings on Sundays?
Receptionist: 7 am to 12 noon.
You: Perfect. See you tomorrow.
```

## Conversation 2: first session with the trainer

```dialogue
Trainer: So, what's your goal?
You: I want to lose about eight kilos and get fitter. I sit at a desk all day.
Trainer: Got it. Any injuries or health problems?
You: I have some lower back pain sometimes, and I'm a bit stiff.
Trainer: Okay, we'll avoid heavy deadlifts for now. We'll start with a 10-minute warm-up on the treadmill.
You: How many days a week should I come?
Trainer: Four days to start: two days strength, two days cardio.
(Later)
Trainer: Now, three sets of twelve on the leg press.
You: Is my form correct? I feel it in my knees.
Trainer: Keep your feet a little higher, and don't lock your knees. Yes, that's better.
You: Thanks. Should I stretch after this?
Trainer: Yes, always cool down and stretch for five minutes.
```

## Gym etiquette phrases

- "**Mind if I use this bench** while you rest?"
- "**Sorry, are you done** with these dumbbells?"
- "**Could you wipe the machine**, please?" (politely) / "**Let me wipe it down** for you."
- "**I'll be quick**, only one more set."

## When things go wrong

- "**I think I've pulled a muscle** in my shoulder. Do you have an ice pack?"
- "**This treadmill isn't working.** The belt keeps stopping."
- "**I want to cancel / freeze** my membership. What's the process?"
