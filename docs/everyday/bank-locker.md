# Bank Locker

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** apply for a bank locker, ask about rent, rules and access, operate it with a joint
holder, and handle problems like a lost key or surrendering the locker.
</div>

!!! info "Rules differ by bank"
    Locker rent, deposits, access rules and agreements depend on your bank and country. Always ask for
    the **written agreement** and read it before signing.

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| safe deposit locker | a locked box in the bank's vault |
| small / medium / large locker | sizes (rent depends on size) |
| annual rent / advance rent | yearly fee |
| fixed deposit (FD) as security | some banks ask for an FD |
| locker agreement | the contract with the bank |
| waiting list | queue when no locker is free |
| joint holder / operate jointly / either or survivor | two people can use it |
| nominee / nomination | who gets access if the holder dies |
| access / operation / visit | opening the locker |
| locker key / master key | your key / the bank's key (both needed) |
| surrender / break open | give it back / force it open (for example, if the key is lost) |

## Questions you'll ask

=== "Applying"

    - **Is a locker available** at this branch? What sizes?
    - **What's the annual rent** for a medium locker?
    - **Do I need a fixed deposit** or a savings account here?
    - **Is there a waiting list?** How long is it?
    - **What documents** do I need?
    - **Can I add my wife as a joint holder**? And a nominee?
    - **Could I read the locker agreement** before I sign?

=== "Using it"

    - **What are the locker operating hours?**
    - **How many visits** are free each year?
    - **Do I need to book** a visit in advance?
    - **Can my joint holder** visit alone?
    - **What is not allowed** inside the locker?
    - **What's the bank's liability** if something is lost or damaged?

## Conversation 1: applying for a locker

```dialogue
You: Good morning. I'd like to apply for a locker at this branch.
Officer: Good morning. Do you have an account with us?
You: Yes, a savings account. Here's my account number.
Officer: Thank you. We have medium lockers available. Small ones have a waiting list.
You: What's the annual rent for a medium locker?
Officer: ₹4,000 plus GST, paid in advance.
You: Do I need a fixed deposit as well?
Officer: Yes, an FD covering three years' rent, as security.
You: Okay. Can my wife be a joint holder, and can I add a nominee?
Officer: Yes, both. Please fill in this form, and bring her ID and photographs.
You: Could I take a copy of the locker agreement to read first?
Officer: Of course. Here it is.
You: Thank you. I'll bring everything tomorrow.
```

## Conversation 2: lost locker key

```dialogue
You: Hi. I'm afraid I've lost my locker key. The locker number is 214.
Officer: I'm sorry to hear that. When did you last use it?
You: About two months ago. I've searched the whole house, but I can't find it.
Officer: You'll need to give a written request and an indemnity, and possibly a police report.
You: Okay. What happens next?
Officer: We'll arrange for the locker to be opened in your presence by the vendor, and the lock will be changed.
You: Is there a charge for that?
Officer: Yes, the charges for breaking it open and replacing the lock. About ₹5,000.
You: Understood. How long will it take?
Officer: About a week. We'll call you with a date.
You: Thank you. Could you give me the list of documents I need to submit?
```

## Other useful sentences

- "**I'd like to operate** my locker, number 214."
- "**I'd like to surrender** my locker. What's the process for the FD and the key?"
- "**I haven't received** the rent debit notice. Has it been paid?"
- "**Could you update the nominee** on my locker?"
