# Phone Calls

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Phone calls are harder than face-to-face talk because you can't see the other person.** Prepare 3
bullet points, speak slowly, and confirm everything at the end.
</div>

## Before you call

- [ ] Write your **goal**: "Reschedule appointment to Friday."
- [ ] Have **details ready**: booking ID, account number, dates
- [ ] Have **pen & paper** for reference numbers
- [ ] Find a **quiet place**

## Opening the call

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"Hi, **this is Ashish Gupta**. I'm calling about **my booking, reference 4X7Q2**. **I'd like to
change the date** to Friday the 14th."
</div>

## Useful phrases

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| Didn't hear | "Sorry, **the line's not clear**. Could you repeat that?" |
| Spelling | "Could you **spell that** for me?" / "That's G for Goa, U for Umbrella…" |
| Numbers | "Let me **read that back**: 9-8-4-5…" |
| Put on hold | "Sure, **I'll hold**." |
| Wrong department | "Could you **transfer me** to the billing team, please?" |
| Getting nowhere | "I understand. **Could I speak to a supervisor**, please?" |
| Ending | "**Just to confirm**: the appointment is Friday at 3 pm, ref 4X7Q2. Thank you!" |

## Work calls

- Start: "Hi Rahul, **is now a good time?** It'll take 5 minutes."
- Unexpected call: "**Can I call you back in 10 minutes?** I'm just finishing something."
- End: "Great, **I'll send a quick email** summarising what we agreed."

## Leaving a voicemail

```text
"Hi, this is Ashish Gupta from XYZ Technologies. I'm calling about the invoice for October.
Please call me back on 98450-12345. Again, that's 98450-12345. Thanks!"
```

Name → reason → number (said twice, slowly) → thanks.
