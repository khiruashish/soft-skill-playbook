# On the Train

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Railway stations are busy, loud and quick.** Ask early, confirm twice, and keep your ticket and ID
ready. These phrases work in India and abroad.
</div>

## At the ticket counter / enquiry

**You can ask:**

- "**One ticket to Pune**, please. Second AC / sleeper / general?" *(India)*
- "**A return ticket to Manchester**, please. Off-peak if possible." *(UK)*
- "**What time is the next train** to Chennai?"
- "Is it a **direct train**, or do I need to **change**?"
- "**Which platform** does it leave from?"
- "How long does the journey take?"
- "Is there a **cheaper option** / **railcard / senior discount**?"
- "Can I **cancel or change** this ticket later? Is there a fee?"

## At the station

| Ask | When |
|---|---|
| "Excuse me, is this the right platform for the **Shatabdi to Mysore**?" | Before boarding |
| "Has the **platform changed**?" | When boards/announcements are confusing |
| "Is the train **running late**?" | Check delays |
| "Where does **coach B3** stop on the platform?" | Long trains (India) |
| "Where's the **waiting room / cloakroom / lift**?" | Facilities |
| "Does this train **stop at** Kalyan?" | Confirm stops |

## On the train

=== "Your seat"

    - "Excuse me, I think **this is my seat**: 32, coach S4." (show ticket politely)
    - "Would you mind **swapping seats**? I'd like to sit with my family."
    - "Is **this seat taken**?" *(unreserved trains)*
    - "Could you help me **lift my bag** onto the rack?"

=== "Co-passengers"

    - "Do you mind if I **open / close the window**?"
    - "Could you please **lower your voice / use headphones**? I'm trying to sleep."
    - "Could you **keep an eye on my bag** for two minutes?"
    - "Which **station is next**?"

=== "Ticket examiner (TTE / conductor)"

    | They ask / say | You say |
    |---|---|
    | "Ticket, please." | "Here you go." (ticket + ID) |
    | "Your ticket is **waitlisted / RAC**." | "Is there any **berth available** now? Could you allot one, please?" |
    | "You're in the wrong coach." | "Sorry, which coach should I go to?" |
    | "This ticket isn't valid on this train." | "I'm sorry, I didn't realise. **What are my options?** Can I pay the difference?" |

## When things go wrong

=== "Missed the train"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Hi, **I just missed the 6:15 train** to Bangalore. **Can I use this ticket on the next one**, or
    do I need a new ticket? Can I get a partial refund?"
    </div>

=== "Train cancelled / very late"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "The 9 am train is **cancelled**. **Is there an alternative train or bus?** How do I claim a
    **refund**?"
    </div>

=== "Lost item"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I **left my laptop bag** on the train that arrived at platform 4 at 10:30, coach A1, seat 22.
    It's a **black backpack**. **Where is the lost-property office**, or who can I report it to?"
    </div>

## Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Platform | Where the train stops |
| Berth (upper / middle / lower / side) | Sleeping place *(India)* |
| Waitlisted (WL) / RAC | Not yet confirmed / shared seat *(India)* |
| Tatkal | Last-minute booking quota *(India)* |
| Single / Return | One-way / round trip *(UK)* |
| Peak / Off-peak | Busy (expensive) / quieter (cheaper) times |
| Change at… | Get off and take another train at… |
| Terminus | Last station of the line |
| Fare | Ticket price |
