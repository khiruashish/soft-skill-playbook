## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| neighbour / next door / upstairs / downstairs | people near you |
| residents' welfare association (RWA) / society | building management group |
| secretary / committee / AGM | RWA roles / annual meeting |
| common area / clubhouse / lift / parking | shared spaces |
| maintenance charges | monthly fee for the building |
| complaint / request / notice | formal messages |

## Friendly phrases

- "Hi, **I'm Ashish from 304.** We just moved in."
- "**If you ever need anything**, just knock."
- "**Could I borrow** a ladder for ten minutes?"
- "**Could you collect my parcel** if it comes while I'm out?"
- "**We're having a small get-together** on Saturday. Would you like to come?"

## Complaining politely: the 3 Fs

<div class="ssp-formula">Friendly opening → <em>Fact (what happened)</em> → Fix (a polite request)</div>

| ❌ Rude | ✅ Polite |
|---|---|
| "Your music is too loud. Stop it." | "Hi! Sorry to bother you. The music is quite loud in our flat, and my kids are trying to sleep. Could you turn it down a little after 10?" |
| "Don't park in my spot." | "Hi, I think your car is in my parking spot, number 12. Could you move it when you get a chance?" |

## Conversation 1: noise from upstairs

```dialogue
You: Hi, I'm Ashish from 304, just below you. Sorry to disturb you.
Neighbour: Oh, hi! No problem. Is everything okay?
You: Yes, mostly. It's just that we hear a lot of furniture moving late at night, around 11 or 12.
Neighbour: Oh, I'm sorry! We've been rearranging the rooms. I didn't realise it was so loud.
You: No worries. My daughter has exams, so she sleeps early. Could you avoid it after 10?
Neighbour: Of course. We'll put felt pads under the chairs too.
You: That's really kind of you. Thank you!
Neighbour: Thanks for telling us nicely.
```

## Conversation 2: speaking at the society meeting

```dialogue
Secretary: Any other points from residents?
You: Yes, I'd like to raise one point about parking.
Secretary: Please go ahead.
You: Many visitors are parking in residents' spots, especially on weekends. Last Sunday, three of us couldn't park.
Resident: That's true. It's happened to me too.
You: I'd like to suggest two things: visitor parking stickers, and the security guard asking visitors to use the visitor area.
Secretary: That sounds reasonable. Do we have a cost estimate for the stickers?
You: I checked. It's about ₹2,000 for 500 stickers. I'm happy to coordinate it.
Secretary: Great. Let's vote on it. All in favour?
```
