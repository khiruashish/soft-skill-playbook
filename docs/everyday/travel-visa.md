# Travel Visa

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** understand visa types, ask the right questions about documents and timelines, and
answer visa-interview questions clearly, briefly and honestly. You'll need all of this before you
book the flight.
</div>

!!! info "Rules differ by country"
    Visa types, documents, fees and processing times change often and depend on the country you're
    visiting and your passport. Always check the **official embassy or consulate website**, or the
    official visa application centre, before you apply. This page is about **communication**.

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tourist / business / work / student / transit visa | visa types |
| single-entry / multiple-entry | enter once / many times |
| validity / duration of stay | how long the visa is valid / how long you may stay each visit |
| embassy / consulate / visa application centre (VAC) | where visas are handled |
| appointment / biometrics | booked slot / fingerprints and photo |
| invitation letter / sponsor | a letter from the host / person or company paying |
| itinerary / travel insurance / hotel booking | travel plan documents |
| bank statements / ITR / salary slips | proof of funds and income |
| no-objection certificate (NOC) / leave letter | letter from your employer |
| approved / refused / administrative processing | visa results |

## Questions you'll ask

=== "Before applying"

    - **Which visa type** do I need for a two-day client meeting?
    - **What documents** do I need? Is there a **checklist**?
    - **How long does processing take?** Can I apply for **priority** processing?
    - **How early can I apply** before my travel date?
    - **Do I need an appointment** for biometrics?
    - **Do I need travel insurance**? What's the minimum cover?
    - **How much is the fee**, and how do I pay?

=== "At the visa centre"

    - **Where do I submit** my documents?
    - **Do you need the originals** or only copies?
    - **Can I keep my passport** while the visa is processed?
    - **How will I know** when the passport is ready to collect?
    - **Can someone else collect** my passport?

## Visa interview: questions you'll be asked

**Golden rule:** answer exactly what's asked, **briefly and honestly**, and have documents ready.

| Officer asks | Good answer |
|---|---|
| What's the purpose of your trip? | I'm attending a three-day product workshop at our client's office in Berlin. |
| Who is paying for the trip? | My company is paying. Here's the invitation and the letter from my employer. |
| How long will you stay? | Five days, from the 10th to the 15th of March. |
| Where will you stay? | At the Hotel Adlon, near the client's office. Here's the booking. |
| What do you do? | I'm a senior software engineer at XYZ Technologies in Bengaluru. I've been there for six years. |
| Why should we believe you'll return? | I have a permanent job, my family lives in Bengaluru, and I have a return ticket for the 15th. |
| Have you travelled abroad before? | Yes, to Singapore in 2023 and to Dubai in 2024, both for work. |
| Do you have relatives there? | No, I don't. / Yes, my cousin lives in Munich, but I'm staying at the hotel. |

!!! warning "Don't"
    Memorise long speeches · give extra information that wasn't asked for · guess dates or numbers ·
    say anything you can't prove. If you don't understand, say: **"Sorry, could you repeat the question?"**

## Conversation 1: at the visa application centre

```dialogue
Staff: Good morning. Appointment letter and passport, please.
You: Good morning. Here you are. I'm applying for a business visa for Germany.
Staff: Do you have the document checklist?
You: Yes. Everything is in this folder, in the same order as the checklist.
Staff: Thank you. The invitation letter is a copy. Do you have the original?
You: The client sent it by email. Is a printed copy acceptable?
Staff: For this category, yes. Please sign here. Next, go to counter 4 for biometrics.
You: Okay. How long does processing usually take?
Staff: About 15 working days. You'll get an SMS when the passport is ready.
You: Can I track the status online?
Staff: Yes, with the reference number on this receipt.
You: Thank you very much.
```

## Conversation 2: the visa interview

```dialogue
Officer: Good morning. What's the purpose of your trip to the United States?
You: Good morning. I'm attending a five-day technical conference in Seattle.
Officer: Who is paying for the trip?
You: My company. Here's the letter from my employer.
Officer: How long have you worked there?
You: Six years. I'm a senior engineer on the payments team.
Officer: Are you married?
You: Yes. My wife and my son live with me in Bengaluru.
Officer: Have you been to the US before?
You: No, this is my first time. I've travelled to Singapore and Dubai for work.
Officer: When are you coming back?
You: On the 22nd of June. My return ticket is booked.
Officer: Okay. Your visa is approved. You'll get your passport back by courier.
You: Thank you!
```

## Follow-up and problems

- "**My application has been in processing for 25 days.** Could you tell me if anything is missing?"
- "**My travel date is next week.** Is it possible to expedite the application?"
- "**My visa was refused.** Could you tell me the reason, and can I reapply?"
- "**There's a spelling mistake** in my name on the visa. How do I get it corrected?"

## Practice

Answer these in one sentence each, for a trip you'd like to take: purpose, length of stay, who pays,
your job, why you'll return.

??? success "Sample answers"
    I'm visiting Japan on holiday with my family. · Ten days, from the 3rd to the 13th of April. · I am,
    from my savings; here are my bank statements. · I'm a software engineer at XYZ Technologies. · I
    have a permanent job and my parents live with me here; my return ticket is booked.

## Practice conversation: asking HR for a visa letter

```dialogue
You: Hi Sunita, I need an employer letter for my German business visa.
HR: Sure. What should it include?
You: My job title, how long I've worked here, my salary, the purpose of the trip, and that the company is paying.
HR: And the dates?
You: Travel from the 10th to the 15th of March. The embassy also needs it on company letterhead, signed.
HR: Okay. I'll have it ready by Thursday.
You: Thank you! Could you also send me a scanned copy by email?
```
