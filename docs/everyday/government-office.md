# Government Office

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** handle passport, ID card, driving licence and other government work: asking which
documents you need, which counter to go to, and following up on applications.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| application / form / appointment slot | what you submit / book |
| documents / proof of address / ID proof | papers you need |
| original / photocopy / self-attested | the real one / copy / copy signed by you |
| token number / counter / window | queue system |
| verification / police verification | checking your details |
| status / acknowledgement / receipt number | where your application is / proof you applied |
| fee / challan | payment |

## Questions you'll ask

- **Which documents do I need** for a passport renewal?
- **Do I need originals**, or are photocopies enough?
- **Which counter** should I go to?
- **How long does it take** to process?
- **How can I check the status** of my application?
- **Is there a fee?** How do I pay it?
- **Can someone else submit it** on my behalf?
- **What should I do if** a document is missing?

## Questions you'll be asked

| They ask | You answer |
|---|---|
| What's your application number? | It's PSK-2026-11823. Here's the printout. |
| Do you have the originals? | Yes, here they are. |
| Has your address changed? | Yes, I moved last year. Here's my rental agreement. |
| Sign here, please. | (Sign.) Is that all? |

## Conversation 1: at the passport office

```dialogue
Officer: Token number?
You: A-142. I'm here for a passport renewal. Here's my appointment letter.
Officer: Old passport and documents, please.
You: Here's the old passport, my Aadhaar card, and the address proof.
Officer: Your address is different from the old passport.
You: Yes, I've moved to Bengaluru. Here's the rental agreement and an electricity bill.
Officer: The electricity bill is in the owner's name. Do you have anything in your name?
You: I have my bank statement with this address. Will that work?
Officer: Yes, that's fine. Please go to counter B for photos and fingerprints.
You: Thank you. How long will it take to get the passport?
Officer: After police verification, about two to three weeks.
You: How can I check the status?
Officer: Online, with your file number. It's on this receipt.
```

## Conversation 2: following up by phone

```dialogue
You: Good morning. I applied for a driving licence renewal a month ago, but the status still says "under process".
Clerk: What's your application number?
You: DL-RN-554210.
Clerk: Let me check… It's waiting for the officer's approval.
You: Is there anything missing from my side?
Clerk: No, the documents are complete.
You: Could you tell me roughly when it will be approved?
Clerk: Within ten working days.
You: Thank you. If there's no update by then, who should I contact?
Clerk: You can email the RTO helpdesk with your application number.
```

## Tips

- Carry **originals + two photocopies** of everything, and **passport-size photos**.
- Write down **token, file and application numbers**.
- Stay **polite and patient**. "**Could you please help me understand** what's missing?" works better than arguing.
