# Yoga Class

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** join a yoga class, tell the teacher about your body, follow spoken instructions, and
ask for easier or harder versions of a pose.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| mat / block / strap | yoga equipment |
| pose / posture / asana | a yoga position |
| inhale / exhale / hold your breath | breathing |
| stretch / bend / twist / balance | movements |
| beginner / intermediate / advanced batch | class levels |
| modification / variation | an easier or different way to do a pose |
| flexible / stiff | body condition |
| meditation / pranayama / relaxation (shavasana) | calm practices |

## Instructions you'll hear

| Teacher says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Inhale**, raise your arms. **Exhale**, fold forward. | Breathe in going up, breathe out going down |
| **Hold** for five breaths. | Stay in the pose |
| **Keep your back straight.** / **Engage your core.** | Don't bend / tighten your stomach muscles |
| **Bend your knees** if it's too much. | Make it easier |
| **Release** and come back to the centre. | Relax and return |
| **Listen to your body.** Don't push. | Stop if it hurts |
| Lie down for **shavasana**. | Relax on your back |

## Questions you'll ask

- **Is this class suitable for beginners?**
- **Do I need to bring my own mat?**
- **What should I wear?**
- **I have a slipped disc / knee problem.** Are there poses I should avoid?
- **Could you show me an easier version?**
- **Am I doing this correctly?**
- **How long should I practise** each day?
- **Should I eat before the class?**

## Conversation 1: joining a yoga class

```dialogue
You: Hi, I'd like to join a yoga class. I'm a complete beginner.
Teacher: Welcome! Our beginner batch is at 6:30 am, Monday to Friday.
You: Great. Do I need to bring my own mat?
Teacher: We have mats, but your own is better for hygiene.
You: Okay. I should mention that I have some lower back pain.
Teacher: Thanks for telling me. I'll give you modifications. Please avoid deep forward bends for now.
You: Should I eat before class?
Teacher: Come on an empty stomach, or eat something light two hours before.
You: Understood. What should I wear?
Teacher: Comfortable, stretchy clothes. No jeans.
```

## Conversation 2: during the class

```dialogue
Teacher: Inhale, raise both arms. Exhale, bend forward slowly.
You: Sorry, I can't reach my toes.
Teacher: That's fine. Bend your knees a little and hold your shins.
You: Like this?
Teacher: Yes, perfect. Keep your back long. Hold for five breaths.
(Later)
Teacher: Now come into tree pose. Balance on your right leg.
You: I keep losing my balance.
Teacher: Put your foot on your ankle instead of your thigh, and look at one point in front of you.
You: Oh, that's much easier.
Teacher: Good. Release. Now lie down for shavasana and relax.
```

## After class

- "**Thank you, that was relaxing.**"
- "**My hamstrings feel tight.** Is that normal?"
- "**Can you suggest** a few poses to do at home before work?"
