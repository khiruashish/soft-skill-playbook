# School & Parent-Teacher Meeting

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** talk to schools about admissions, discuss your child's progress with teachers, raise
concerns respectfully, and agree on how to help at home.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| admission / entrance test / interview | joining a school |
| class teacher / subject teacher / principal | school staff |
| PTM (parent-teacher meeting) | regular progress meeting |
| report card / marks / grades / assessment | results |
| homework / project / assignment | work at home |
| behaviour / attention / participation | how the child acts in class |
| extracurricular activities | sports, music, clubs |
| fee structure / transport / uniform | admin topics |

## Questions you'll ask

=== "Admissions"

    - **What is the admission process** for Class 3?
    - **Which board** do you follow (CBSE / ICSE / IB)?
    - **What's the class size?** / student–teacher ratio?
    - **What's the fee structure?** Are there any other charges?
    - **Is there school transport** from our area?

=== "PTM"

    - **How is she doing** in class overall?
    - **Which subjects** does he find difficult?
    - **How is his behaviour** with other children?
    - **What can we do at home** to help?
    - **Is she participating** in class?
    - **How can I contact you** if I have a concern?

## Conversation 1: parent-teacher meeting

```dialogue
Teacher: Good morning, Mr. Gupta. Please sit down.
You: Good morning, ma'am. Thank you. How is Aarav doing this term?
Teacher: He's doing well in maths and science. He's very curious.
You: That's good to hear. What about English?
Teacher: His reading is good, but his writing needs work. He makes spelling mistakes and doesn't finish his sentences.
You: I see. What can we do at home to help?
Teacher: Ask him to write a short diary every day, five or six lines. And read together for 15 minutes.
You: We can do that. How is his behaviour in class?
Teacher: He's friendly, but sometimes he talks during lessons.
You: I'll speak to him about that. Could we check his progress again in a month?
Teacher: Of course. You can email me any time.
You: Thank you so much for your time, ma'am.
```

## Conversation 2: raising a concern

```dialogue
You: Ma'am, I'd like to discuss something that's worrying us.
Teacher: Of course, please tell me.
You: Over the last two weeks, Aarav has said that some older boys are teasing him on the school bus.
Teacher: I'm sorry to hear that. Did he tell you their names?
You: He mentioned two boys from Class 6. He's become quiet and doesn't want to take the bus.
Teacher: Thank you for telling us. I'll speak to the transport in-charge and the Class 6 teacher today.
You: I appreciate that. Could you let me know what action is taken?
Teacher: Yes, I'll call you by Friday.
You: Thank you. We just want him to feel safe.
```
