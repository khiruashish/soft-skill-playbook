# Home Repairs & Services

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** explain a problem to a plumber, electrician or technician, agree on the cost before
work starts, and give clear instructions to house help.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| leak / drip / blocked / clogged | water problems |
| short circuit / tripped / no power / spark | electrical problems |
| geyser / water heater / RO / purifier | appliances |
| service / repair / replace / spare part | the work |
| visiting charge / labour charge | fee for coming / for work |
| warranty / AMC (annual maintenance contract) | covered repairs |
| house help / cook / driver | domestic staff |

## Describing the problem

<div class="ssp-formula">What + <em>where</em> + since when + what you've tried</div>

- "The **kitchen tap** is **leaking**. It **started yesterday**. I **tightened it**, but it still drips."
- "The **bedroom AC** isn't cooling. It **turns on**, but the air is **warm**. It **was serviced** six months ago."
- "The **power trips** every time we switch on the **geyser**."

## Questions you'll ask

- **What's the visiting charge?**
- **Can you tell me the total cost before you start?**
- **What's the problem?** **Can it be repaired**, or does it need replacing?
- **Is the spare part original?** **Is there a warranty** on the repair?
- **How long will it take?**
- **Can you come** tomorrow morning between 9 and 11?
- **Can I pay by UPI?** **Could I have a bill?**

## Conversation 1: booking an AC repair

```dialogue
Agent: CoolCare service, how can I help?
You: Hi, my split AC isn't cooling. It runs, but the air is warm.
Agent: Which brand and how old is it?
You: It's a Daikin, about three years old.
Agent: When was it last serviced?
You: About eight months ago.
Agent: It may need a gas refill. The visiting charge is ₹399, and that's adjusted in the final bill.
You: How much is a gas refill, roughly?
Agent: Between ₹2,500 and ₹3,500, depending on the leak.
You: Okay. Can the technician come tomorrow between 10 and 12?
Agent: Yes, I've booked it. The technician will call you before coming.
You: Thank you.
```

## Conversation 2: with the technician

```dialogue
Technician: The gas is very low. There's a small leak near the pipe joint.
You: Can you fix the leak, or will you only refill the gas?
Technician: I'll fix the joint first, then refill.
You: What will the total cost be?
Technician: ₹3,200, including the visiting charge.
You: Is there a warranty on the work?
Technician: Three months on the leak repair.
You: Okay, please go ahead. Could you also clean the filters?
Technician: Yes, no extra charge.
You: Great. Please send me the bill on WhatsApp after you finish.
```

## Instructions to house help (clear and respectful)

- "**Could you please** clean the balcony **today**? **Also**, the bedsheets need to be washed."
- "**Please don't use** this cleaner on the wooden table. **Use** the cloth instead."
- "**We'll be away** from Friday to Monday, so **you don't need to come**. Your salary won't be cut."
- "**Thank you**, the kitchen looks great."
