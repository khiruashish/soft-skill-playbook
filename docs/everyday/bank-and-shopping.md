# Bank & Shopping

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**When money is involved, be clear, confirm details, and get it in writing.**
</div>

## At the bank

| Need | Say |
|---|---|
| Open an account | "I'd like to **open a savings account**. What documents do I need?" |
| Card blocked | "**My card has been blocked** after a failed transaction. Could you help me unblock it?" |
| Wrong charge | "There's a **transaction I don't recognise**: ₹4,500 on the 2nd. I'd like to **raise a dispute**." |
| Transfer | "I need to make an **international transfer**. What are the **charges and exchange rate**?" |
| Loan | "What's the **interest rate** for a home loan, and is it **fixed or floating**?" |
| Fees | "Are there any **hidden charges or minimum balance** requirements?" |

!!! tip
    Always ask: "**Could I get a reference number** for this request?" and "**How long** will it take?"

## Shopping

- "I'm **just looking**, thanks."
- "Do you have this **in a bigger / smaller size**? In **blue**?"
- "**Can I try this on?** Where's the fitting room?"
- "**Is there a discount** on this?" / "Is this the **final price**?"
- "What's the **return / exchange policy**?"
- "Does it come with a **warranty**?"

## Returns & complaints

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"Hi, I bought these headphones **last week** and **the left side stopped working**. Here's the
receipt. **I'd like a replacement or a refund**, please."
</div>

If it's not resolved: "**Could I speak to the manager**, please?" Stay calm and polite, and repeat
the facts.

## Bargaining (local markets)

1. "**How much is this?**"
2. "Hmm, that's a bit much. **Could you do ₹400?**"
3. "If I buy two, **what's your best price?**"
4. Smile and walk away politely if it's too high: "Thanks, I'll think about it."
