## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| entry ticket / admission | ticket to go in |
| guided tour / audio guide | tour with a guide / recorded guide |
| opening hours / timings | when it's open |
| heritage site / monument / fort / temple | historic places |
| souvenir | a gift to remember the place |
| viewpoint | place with a good view |
| sunrise / sunset point | best place to watch the sun |
| itinerary | your travel plan |

## Questions you'll ask

- **What are the timings?** / **What time does it close?**
- **How much is the entry ticket?** Is there a **separate ticket for cameras**?
- **Is there a guided tour?** How long does it take? **In English?**
- **How old is** this fort? **Who built** it?
- **What's the best time to visit?**
- **Could you take a photo of us**, please?
- **Can I take photos inside?**
- **What else should we see** nearby?
- **How do we get to** the sunset point from here?

## Conversation 1: hiring a guide at a fort

```dialogue
Guide: Hello sir, do you want a guide? I speak English and Hindi.
You: Maybe. How long is the tour, and how much do you charge?
Guide: One and a half hours. ₹800 for your group.
You: Is that for all four of us?
Guide: Yes, for the whole group.
You: Okay. Could you also show us the best spots for photos?
Guide: Of course. This fort was built in the 16th century. The palace inside is beautiful.
You: Interesting! Who built it?
Guide: Raja Man Singh. Come, let's start from the main gate.
You: Great, let's go.
```

## Conversation 2: asking a stranger for a photo

```dialogue
You: Excuse me, sorry to bother you. Could you take a photo of us, please?
Tourist: Sure! Portrait or landscape?
You: Landscape, please, with the lake behind us.
Tourist: Okay… one, two, three! Let me take one more.
You: Thank you so much! Would you like me to take one of you too?
Tourist: Oh, yes please! That's kind of you.
You: Where are you visiting from?
Tourist: From Germany. It's our first time in India.
You: Welcome! Have you tried the local food yet?
Tourist: Not yet. Any suggestions?
You: Try the dal baati at the restaurant near the gate. It's famous here.
```

## When things go wrong

- "**We've lost our way.** How do we get back to the main entrance?"
- "**The guide is asking for more money** than we agreed."
- "**Is this place safe** after dark?"
- "**Where's the nearest washroom / drinking water?**"
- "**I think I dropped my phone** somewhere near the steps. Has anyone handed one in?"
