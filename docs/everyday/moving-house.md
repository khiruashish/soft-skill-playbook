## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| packers and movers / relocation company | people who pack and move your things |
| survey / quote / estimate | inspection / price offer |
| packing material / bubble wrap / cartons | what they pack with |
| loading / unloading / unpacking | stages of the move |
| transit insurance | cover for damage during the move |
| inventory list | list of every item and box |
| fragile / handle with care | breaks easily |
| shifting / relocation | moving (Indian English: "shifting") |

## Questions to ask before booking

- **Can you do a survey** of the flat before giving a quote?
- **What's included** in the price: packing, loading, transport, unloading, unpacking?
- **Are there any extra charges** for stairs, no lift, long carry or AC removal?
- **Is transit insurance included?** What does it cover?
- **Will you give an inventory list** with box numbers?
- **How many people** will come, and how long will it take?
- **What happens if something is damaged?**
- **Do you need full payment in advance**, or after delivery?

## Conversation 1: getting a quote

```dialogue
Agent: Good morning, SafeShift Movers.
You: Hi. I'm moving from a 2BHK in Whitefield to Hebbal next month. I'd like a quote.
Agent: Sure. What's the date?
You: The 28th, a Saturday. Can someone come and survey the flat first?
Agent: Yes, our surveyor can come on Wednesday evening.
(After the survey)
Agent: The quote is ₹18,500, including packing, loading, transport, unloading and unpacking.
You: Does that include insurance and taking down the AC?
Agent: AC removal and fitting is ₹1,500 extra. Transit insurance is 3% of the declared value.
You: Okay. If I book this week, could you include the AC work in the ₹18,500?
Agent: Let me check with my manager… Yes, we can do that.
You: Great. Please email me the quote with everything included, and the payment terms.
```

## Conversation 2: delivery day problem

```dialogue
You: Hi, the team has unloaded everything, but one box is missing, number 23, and the glass table top is cracked.
Supervisor: I'm sorry. Let me check the truck again.
(Ten minutes later)
Supervisor: We found box 23. It was behind the fridge. But the table top is definitely damaged.
You: It was packed by your team, and it's on the inventory list as "fragile". How do we claim the insurance?
Supervisor: Please take photos now and note it on the delivery receipt before signing.
You: Okay. I'm writing "glass table top cracked" on the receipt. How long does the claim take?
Supervisor: About two weeks. I'll send you the claim form today.
You: Thank you. I'll hold back the final payment for the table until the claim is settled.
```

## Settling in

- (Society office) "**We've just moved into** B-1104. **What are the rules** for parking and deliveries?"
- (Electricity) "**I'd like to transfer** the electricity connection to my name."
- (Internet) "**Can you shift** my broadband connection to my new address?"
- (Neighbour) "Hi, we're the new family in 1104. **Sorry about the noise** from unpacking this week!"

## Moving checklist

- [ ] Quotes from 2–3 companies, **in writing**
- [ ] Photos of valuable items before packing
- [ ] Inventory list checked at loading **and** unloading
- [ ] Address change: bank, Aadhaar, gas, insurance, deliveries, employer
- [ ] Deposit back from the old landlord (see [Renting a Home](renting-a-home.md))
