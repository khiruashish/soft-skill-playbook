# Police & Emergencies

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** stay clear and calm in an emergency: call for help, report a theft or accident, and
answer police questions with exact facts.
</div>

!!! danger "Emergency numbers (India)"
    **112** all emergencies · **100** police · **101** fire · **108** ambulance (most states) ·
    **1930** cyber-fraud helpline. Abroad, check the local number before you travel (911 US, 112 EU, 999 UK).

## The emergency call formula

<div class="ssp-formula">What happened → <em>exact location + landmark</em> → who's hurt / in danger → your name & number</div>

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"**There's been a road accident.** A bike and a car have collided **on Outer Ring Road, near the
Marathahalli bridge, opposite the petrol pump.** **One man is bleeding from the head and isn't
moving.** **My name is Ashish, and my number is 98450 12345.**"
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FIR / complaint / report | the official police record |
| theft / stolen / pickpocketed / snatched | taking your things |
| accident / collision / hit-and-run | road incidents |
| witness | someone who saw it |
| fraud / scam / OTP / unauthorised transaction | online crime |
| injured / unconscious / bleeding | medical state |

## Questions you'll be asked

| Police ask | You answer |
|---|---|
| What happened? | My phone was snatched by two men on a bike. |
| When and where? | About 8:30 pm today, outside the Indiranagar metro station, exit B. |
| Can you describe them? | Two young men, black helmets, a red bike. I couldn't see the number plate. |
| What was stolen? | An iPhone 14, black cover. Here's the IMEI number from the box. |
| Were there any witnesses? | A shopkeeper saw it. He's still there. |

## Questions you'll ask

- **How do I file an FIR / complaint?**
- **Can I file it online?**
- **Could I have a copy** of the complaint / FIR number?
- **What should I do next?** (block SIM, inform bank)
- **Will you inform me** if you find anything?

## Conversation 1: reporting a stolen phone

```dialogue
Officer: Yes, what's the problem?
You: I'd like to report a theft. My phone was snatched about an hour ago.
Officer: Where did it happen?
You: Outside Indiranagar metro station, exit B, at around 8:30.
Officer: Did you see the person?
You: Two men on a red bike. Both wore black helmets.
Officer: Okay. Which phone, and do you have the IMEI number?
You: An iPhone 14. Here's the IMEI from the bill. I've already blocked my SIM.
Officer: Good. Fill in this form. We'll register the complaint.
You: Could I have a copy with the complaint number? I need it for insurance.
Officer: Yes, you'll get a copy.
You: Thank you, sir.
```

## Conversation 2: online fraud

```dialogue
You: Hello, is this the cyber-fraud helpline? Money was taken from my account without my permission.
Agent: How much, and when?
You: ₹25,000, about twenty minutes ago. I got a call from someone pretending to be from my bank, and I shared an OTP. I realise now it was a scam.
Agent: Have you informed your bank?
You: Not yet. I called you first.
Agent: Call your bank right away and block your card. I'm registering your complaint now. What's the transaction ID?
You: It's UPI ref 6102 9933 4471.
Agent: Your complaint number is CYB-77120. Please also file a report on the cybercrime portal.
You: Thank you. I'll call the bank now.
```

## Stay calm: phrases that help

- "**Please send help quickly.** The address is…"
- "**I'll stay on the line.**"
- "**Is there anything I should do** until the ambulance arrives?"
- "**Sorry, could you repeat that?** I'm a bit shaken."
