## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1BHK / 2BHK | 1 or 2 bedrooms + hall + kitchen |
| furnished / semi-furnished / unfurnished | with furniture / some / none |
| rent / maintenance / deposit / advance | monthly cost / building charges / refundable money |
| broker / brokerage | agent / agent's fee |
| rental agreement / lease | the contract |
| notice period / lock-in period | time to inform before leaving / minimum stay |
| utilities | electricity, water, gas |
| tenant / landlord / owner | renter / owner |

## Questions you'll ask

=== "On the visit"

    - **How much is the rent**, and **is maintenance included**?
    - **How much is the deposit?** Is it fully refundable?
    - **Is there a lock-in period?** What's the notice period?
    - **Is there 24-hour water supply / power backup?**
    - **Is parking included?** For a car or only a bike?
    - **Are pets allowed?** **Are there any restrictions** (e.g. bachelors, non-veg)?
    - **When can I move in?**

=== "During your stay"

    - **The geyser isn't working.** Could you get it repaired?
    - **Who pays for** the repair?
    - **Can I paint** the room / put up shelves?
    - **I'll be moving out** at the end of next month. Here's my notice.
    - **When will I get the deposit back?**

## Conversation 1: visiting a flat

```dialogue
Owner: Come in. This is the 2BHK. It's semi-furnished.
You: It's nice and bright. How much is the rent?
Owner: ₹32,000 a month, plus ₹3,000 maintenance.
You: And the deposit?
Owner: Six months' rent.
You: That's quite high. Most places nearby ask for three months. Would you accept three?
Owner: Hmm, I can do four.
You: Okay, four works. Is there power backup and 24-hour water?
Owner: Yes, both.
You: Is there a lock-in period?
Owner: Eleven-month agreement, with a two-month notice period.
You: Can we add in the agreement that the deposit is returned within 15 days of moving out?
Owner: Sure, that's fair.
You: Great. I'd like to move in on the 1st.
```

## Conversation 2: calling the landlord about a problem

```dialogue
You: Hi Mr. Reddy, this is Ashish from flat 302. Sorry to call on a Sunday.
Owner: No problem. What happened?
You: There's water leaking from the bathroom ceiling. It started last night, and it's getting worse.
Owner: Oh no. Is it from the flat above?
You: I think so. I've put a bucket under it, and I've told the people upstairs.
Owner: Okay. I'll send a plumber tomorrow morning. Will someone be home?
You: I'll work from home tomorrow, so yes. Could you also check who will pay for the repair?
Owner: If it's from the upstairs pipe, they'll pay. Don't worry.
You: Thank you. Please send me the plumber's number.
```

## Before you sign: checklist

- [ ] Rent, maintenance, deposit, brokerage, all **in writing**
- [ ] Lock-in and notice period
- [ ] Who pays for which repairs
- [ ] Photos/video of the flat's condition on move-in day
- [ ] Meter readings noted on day one
