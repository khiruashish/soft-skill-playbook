## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| court / ground / turf / pitch | where you play |
| book a slot / court booking | reserve time to play |
| team / captain / opponent / substitute | people in a game |
| score / win / lose / draw / tie | results |
| serve / rally / smash (badminton) | badminton terms |
| bat / bowl / wicket / boundary / over (cricket) | cricket terms |
| warm up / injury / cramp | before playing / getting hurt |
| match / tournament / league | competitions |

## Questions you'll ask

- **Can I join you?** Do you need one more player?
- **How do I book a court?** Is it ₹ per hour or per person?
- **Do you play every weekend?** What time?
- **Doubles or singles?**
- **Whose serve is it?** / **What's the score?**
- **Did you watch the match last night?**
- **Who do you support** in the IPL?

## Conversation 1: joining a badminton group

```dialogue
You: Hi! Do you play here every Saturday?
Player: Yes, from 7 to 9. We're a group from the apartments nearby.
You: Could I join you? I haven't played in a while, but I'd love to start again.
Player: Sure! We usually play doubles. We split the court booking. It's ₹150 per person.
You: That's fine. Do I need my own racket?
Player: Bring your own if you have one. We provide shuttles.
You: Great. I'll come next Saturday. Is there a WhatsApp group?
Player: Yes, give me your number and I'll add you.
You: It's 98450 12345. Thanks a lot!
```

## Conversation 2: cricket chat at work

```dialogue
Colleague: Did you watch the match last night?
You: Yes! What a finish. I couldn't believe they chased 190.
Colleague: That last over was amazing. Two sixes in a row!
You: I know. I thought they'd lost after the third wicket fell so early.
Colleague: Who do you support?
You: Chennai, always. But I don't mind which team wins as long as it's a good match. What about you?
Colleague: Bengaluru. Maybe this is finally our year!
You: Ha, maybe. Shall we watch the final together at the café downstairs?
Colleague: Great idea. Let's ask the team.
```

## In the game

- "**Good shot!**" / "**Well played!**" / "**Unlucky!**" / "**Nice try!**"
- "**Yours!**" / "**Mine!**" (who takes the shot)
- "**Was that in or out?**"
- "**Let's play one more game.**"
- "**Good game, everyone.** Same time next week?"
