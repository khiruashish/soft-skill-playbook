# Customer Care & Complaints

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** call or chat with customer care for mobile, internet, apps and services, explain the
issue fast, stay calm, escalate politely, and get a complaint number.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| customer care / support / helpline | the service team |
| complaint / ticket / reference number | the record of your issue |
| escalate / supervisor / nodal officer | take it to a higher level |
| recharge / plan / postpaid / prepaid | mobile plans |
| network issue / no signal / slow speed / outage | connection problems |
| technician visit | someone comes to your home |
| refund / reversal / chargeback | money back |
| TAT (turnaround time) | how long they'll take |

## The complaint formula

<div class="ssp-formula">Who you are → <em>what's wrong</em> → since when → what you tried → what you want</div>

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"Hi, my name is Ashish Gupta, and my account number is 7781 2345. **My home internet hasn't been
working since yesterday evening.** The router shows a red light. **I've restarted it twice.** **I'd like
a technician visit today**, please."
</div>

## Questions you'll ask

- **Could I have the complaint number**, please?
- **How long will it take** to fix? / **What's the TAT?**
- **Will I get a credit** for the days the service didn't work?
- **Could you escalate this**, please? / **Could I speak to your supervisor?**
- **Can you send me** the details by email/SMS?
- **Why was I charged** ₹499 extra this month?

## Conversation 1: internet down

```dialogue
Agent: Thank you for calling FastNet. My name is Rohit. How may I help you?
You: Hi Rohit. My home internet has been down since 8 pm yesterday. The account is in the name of Ashish Gupta.
Agent: May I have your registered mobile number?
You: It's 98450 12345.
Agent: Thank you. Have you tried restarting the router?
You: Yes, twice. The "internet" light is red.
Agent: I can see an outage in your area. It should be fixed in four hours.
You: I work from home, so this is urgent. Could you give me a complaint number?
Agent: Sure, it's FN-552190.
You: Thank you. Will I get a credit for the downtime?
Agent: Yes, the credit will be added automatically to your next bill.
You: Great. If it isn't fixed by 2 pm, I'll call back with this number.
```

## Conversation 2: escalating a wrong charge

```dialogue
You: Hi, I called twice about a wrong charge of ₹1,200 on my bill, but it hasn't been fixed.
Agent: I'm sorry. Could you share the complaint number?
You: It's MB-81234. I was charged for international roaming, but I haven't travelled abroad.
Agent: I can see the complaint. It's still under review.
You: It's been ten days. I'd like this escalated, please. Could I speak to a supervisor?
Agent: Please hold, I'll transfer you.
Supervisor: Hello, I'm Anjali, the supervisor. I've checked: the charge was a system error.
You: Thank you. When will the amount be reversed?
Supervisor: Within 48 hours. You'll get an SMS confirmation.
You: Could you also confirm it by email, please?
Supervisor: Yes, I'll send it now.
```

## Staying calm (and effective)

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Try |
|---|---|
| Shouting at the agent | "I know it's not your fault. I just need this fixed today." |
| Explaining the whole story again | "Here's my complaint number, and here's what's still pending." |
| Ending the call without a number | "Before we finish, could I have the reference number?" |
