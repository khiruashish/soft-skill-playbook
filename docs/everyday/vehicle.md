# Petrol Pump & Vehicle Service

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** fill fuel and air, book a service, describe a problem with your car or bike, approve
repairs, and get help in a breakdown.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| petrol / diesel / CNG / EV charging | fuel types |
| fill it up / full tank | fill completely |
| tyre pressure / puncture / flat tyre | tyre issues |
| service / general service / oil change | routine maintenance |
| brakes / clutch / battery / engine / AC | car parts |
| noise / vibration / warning light | symptoms |
| estimate / job card / pickup and drop | service terms |
| breakdown / tow truck / roadside assistance | when the vehicle stops |

## At the petrol pump

- "**₹1,000 petrol**, please." / "**Fill it up**, please." / "**Full tank**."
- "**Could you check the tyre pressure?** **32** in the front and **34** at the back."
- "**Please show me the zero** on the meter before you start."
- "**Can I pay by card / UPI?**"
- "**Where can I get the puncture fixed?**"

## Describing a problem

<div class="ssp-formula">What you notice → <em>when it happens</em> → since when</div>

- "There's a **squeaking noise** when I **brake**. It **started last week**."
- "The **engine warning light** came on **this morning**."
- "The car **pulls to the left** when I drive straight."
- "The **AC isn't cooling** properly, especially in **traffic**."
- "The **bike doesn't start** in the **morning**; it takes four or five kicks."

## Conversation 1: booking a car service

```dialogue
Advisor: Good morning, AutoCare service centre.
You: Hi. I'd like to book a general service for my Honda City. It's due at 30,000 kilometres.
Advisor: Sure. Any specific problems?
You: Yes, there's a squeaking noise when I brake, and the AC is weaker than before.
Advisor: Noted. We'll check the brake pads and the AC gas. Is tomorrow at 10 okay?
You: Yes. Do you offer pickup and drop?
Advisor: Yes, it's free for general service.
You: Great. Could you call me before doing any extra work? I'd like to approve the estimate first.
Advisor: Of course. We'll send the estimate on WhatsApp.
You: Thank you.
```

## Conversation 2: approving repairs

```dialogue
Advisor: Sir, we've checked the car. The front brake pads are worn out and need replacing.
You: How much will that cost?
Advisor: ₹3,800 for the pads, including labour. And the AC needs a gas top-up, ₹1,500.
You: Is the brake replacement urgent?
Advisor: Yes, they're at 10%. It's a safety issue.
You: Okay, please go ahead with both. Is there a warranty on the brake pads?
Advisor: Six months or 10,000 kilometres.
You: Fine. When will the car be ready?
Advisor: By 5 pm today.
You: Please send me the final bill before delivery. Thanks.
```
