# Grocery & Vegetable Market

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** buy groceries at a supermarket, kirana store or vegetable market: quantities, prices,
freshness, home delivery and bargaining.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| kilo (kg) / half a kilo / 250 grams | weights |
| a dozen (12) / half a dozen (6) | eggs, bananas |
| a packet / a bottle / a bunch / a loaf | units (a bunch of coriander, a loaf of bread) |
| fresh / ripe / raw / stale / rotten | condition of food |
| in stock / out of stock | available / not available |
| MRP / discount / offer / buy one get one | prices and deals |
| expiry date / best before | last safe date |
| aisle | a row in a supermarket |
| bill / receipt / carry bag | what you get at the end |

## Questions you'll ask

=== "Supermarket"

    - Excuse me, **where can I find** the rice / cooking oil / detergent?
    - **Do you have** brown bread? **When will it be back in stock?**
    - **Is this on offer?** / **Is there any discount** on this?
    - **What's the expiry date** on this milk?
    - **Can I get a carry bag?** How much is it?
    - **Do you do home delivery?** Is there a minimum order?

=== "Vegetable market / kirana"

    - **How much are** the tomatoes? / **How much for a kilo** of onions?
    - **Are these fresh?** When did they come in?
    - **Can I choose them myself?**
    - **Give me half a kilo**, please. / **A quarter kilo** of green chillies.
    - **Can you do ₹40?** / **What's your best price?**
    - **Can you add some coriander**, please? (often free)
    - **Can I pay by UPI?**

## Conversation 1: at the vegetable market

```dialogue
You: Bhaiya, how much are the tomatoes?
Vendor: ₹40 a kilo.
You: And the onions?
Vendor: ₹35. Very good quality, fresh this morning.
You: Okay. One kilo of tomatoes and two kilos of onions. Can I choose the tomatoes myself?
Vendor: Sure, take this basket.
You: Thanks. And half a kilo of beans. Are these fresh?
Vendor: Yes, very fresh. That's ₹150 total.
You: Can you do ₹140? And add some coriander, please.
Vendor: Okay, ₹140. Here's the coriander.
You: Thank you! I'll pay by UPI.
```

## Conversation 2: supermarket, out of stock and billing

```dialogue
You: Excuse me, where can I find the oats?
Staff: Aisle 6, next to the breakfast cereals.
You: I checked, but the 1-kilo pack is out of stock. Do you have any in the back?
Staff: Let me check… Sorry, it'll come tomorrow. We have the 500-gram packs.
You: Okay, I'll take two of those. Is there an offer on them?
Staff: Yes, 10% off on two.
(At the billing counter)
Cashier: Do you have a membership card?
You: No, I don't. Do I need one for the discount?
Cashier: No, the discount is for everyone. Do you need a carry bag?
You: No, thanks. I've brought my own.
Cashier: Your total is ₹1,240.
You: Here's my card. Could I also get the bill on WhatsApp?
```

## When things go wrong

- "**This milk has expired**. The date was yesterday."
- "**I think you've charged me twice** for the rice."
- "**The price on the shelf is ₹99**, but the bill says ₹120."
- "**Some of the eggs are broken.** Could I exchange them?"
- "My online order is **missing two items**."
