# Doctor & Pharmacy

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Being precise with a doctor is like writing a good bug report:** symptoms, when it started, how bad it
is, and what makes it better or worse.
</div>

## The "health bug report"

```text
What:      sore throat and fever (101°F)
Since:     2 days, started Tuesday night
Severity:  pain 6/10 when swallowing
Pattern:   worse at night
Tried:     paracetamol — helps for ~4 hours
Other:     no allergies; I take blood-pressure medicine (amlodipine)
```

## At the doctor's

**Describing symptoms:**

- "I've had **a headache for three days**."
- "It's a **sharp / dull / throbbing / burning** pain."
- "It **hurts when** I bend down / swallow / breathe deeply."
- "I feel **dizzy / nauseous / tired** all the time."
- "I'm **allergic to** penicillin."

**Questions to ask the doctor:**

- "**What do you think is causing this?**"
- "Is it **serious**? Is it **contagious**?"
- "**How often** should I take this medicine, and **for how long**?"
- "Are there any **side effects** I should watch for?"
- "Can I **work / travel / exercise**?"
- "**When should I come back** if it doesn't get better?"
- "Do I need any **tests**?"

## At the pharmacy

- "I have a **prescription** for this."
- "Do you have something **for a cough / cold / acidity**?"
- "Is this **safe to take with** my blood-pressure medicine?"
- "Should I take it **before or after food**?"
- "Is there a **generic / cheaper** version?"

## Emergencies

!!! danger "Emergency phrases"
    "**I need an ambulance.** Someone has collapsed at [exact address / landmark]."
    "**Please call a doctor**, it's urgent."
    "**Where is the nearest hospital / emergency room?**"

    India: **112** (all emergencies) · **108** (ambulance in many states). Abroad: learn the local
    emergency number before you travel (e.g. 911 US, 112 EU, 999 UK).

## Practice conversations

### At the doctor's

```dialogue
Doctor: Good morning. What brings you here today?
You: Good morning, doctor. I've had a sore throat and a fever for two days.
Doctor: How high is the fever?
You: It was 101 last night. I took a paracetamol, and it came down for a few hours.
Doctor: Any cough or body pain?
You: A dry cough, and some body ache.
Doctor: Let me check your throat… It's an infection. I'll prescribe an antibiotic for five days.
You: Should I take it before or after food?
Doctor: After food, twice a day. Drink warm water and rest.
You: Can I go to work?
Doctor: Rest for two days. Come back if the fever doesn't go down by Friday.
You: Thank you, doctor.
```

### At the pharmacy

```dialogue
You: Hi, I have a prescription for these medicines.
Pharmacist: Let me see… We have both. Do you want the full course of the antibiotic?
You: Yes, the full five days. Is there a generic version of the cough syrup?
Pharmacist: Yes, it's cheaper and works the same way.
You: Okay, I'll take that. Can I take the syrup with the antibiotic?
Pharmacist: Yes, that's fine. Just keep two hours between the syrup and any antacid.
You: Thank you. Could I have a bill for insurance?
Pharmacist: Sure, here it is.
```
