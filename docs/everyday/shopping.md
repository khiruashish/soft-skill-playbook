# Shopping & Returns

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** shop for clothes and electronics: sizes, colours, trial rooms, warranties, bargaining,
and returning or exchanging something that's wrong.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| size (S / M / L / XL) / fit (tight, loose) | for clothes |
| trial room / fitting room | where you try clothes on |
| exchange / return / refund | swap it / give it back / money back |
| receipt / bill / invoice | proof of purchase |
| warranty / guarantee | free repair/replacement for a period |
| EMI / no-cost EMI | pay in monthly parts |
| cash on delivery (COD) | pay when it arrives |
| out of stock / pre-order | not available / order before release |

## Questions you'll ask

=== "Clothes"

    - **Do you have this in** a medium / in blue?
    - **Can I try this on?** Where's the trial room?
    - **Does it come in** other colours?
    - **It's a bit tight.** Do you have a bigger size?
    - **Is this cotton?** Can I wash it in a machine?

=== "Electronics"

    - **What's the difference between** these two models?
    - **How long is the warranty?** Is it from the brand or the shop?
    - **Is there an EMI option?** Is it no-cost EMI?
    - **Can you install** it at home? Is installation free?
    - **What's the best price** you can give me?

=== "Returns"

    - **What's your return policy?**
    - **Can I exchange it** for a different size?
    - **Can I get a refund** instead of store credit?
    - **How long does the refund take?**

## Conversation 1: buying a shirt

```dialogue
Staff: Hello! Can I help you find something?
You: Yes, I'm looking for a formal shirt for an interview.
Staff: These are our new cotton shirts. What size are you?
You: Usually a 40, or a large.
Staff: Here's a white one in 40. Would you like to try it on?
You: Yes, please. Where's the trial room?
Staff: Just there, on the right.
(A few minutes later)
You: It's a little tight on the shoulders. Do you have a 42?
Staff: Yes, here you go. It also comes in light blue.
You: The 42 fits well. I'll take the white one and the light blue one.
Staff: Great. Anything else?
You: No, that's all. What's your exchange policy, just in case?
Staff: You can exchange within 15 days with the bill and tags.
```

## Conversation 2: returning headphones

```dialogue
You: Hi. I bought these headphones here last week, and the left side has stopped working.
Staff: I'm sorry to hear that. Do you have the bill?
You: Yes, here it is. It was the 4th of this month.
Staff: Thank you. Let me test them… Yes, the left side isn't working.
You: I'd like a replacement, please. Or a refund, if you don't have the same model.
Staff: We have the same model in stock. I'll replace them for you.
You: Thank you. Will the warranty start again from today?
Staff: No, it continues from the original date, but the new pair is fully covered.
You: Okay. Could you write that on the bill?
Staff: Sure.
```

## When things go wrong

- "**The size is wrong** in my online order. How do I return it?"
- "**The colour is different** from the photo."
- "**It was damaged** when it arrived. Here are the photos."
- "**You said** I could return it within 15 days."
- "**Could I speak to the store manager**, please?"
