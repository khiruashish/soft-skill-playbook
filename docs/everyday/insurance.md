# Insurance

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** ask the right questions before buying health, life, motor or travel insurance, make a

!!! info "Read the policy wording"
    Cover, exclusions and claim rules depend on the insurer and the plan. Before buying, ask for the
    **policy wording and exclusions in writing**, and check claims with the **insurer's official helpline**.

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| premium | what you pay (monthly / yearly) |
| sum insured / cover | the maximum the insurer pays |
| policyholder / insured / nominee | who owns it / who's covered / who receives money |
| term plan / health / motor / travel insurance | policy types |
| waiting period | time before some illnesses are covered |
| pre-existing disease (PED) | illness you had before buying |
| exclusions | what is NOT covered |
| co-pay / deductible | your share of the bill |
| cashless / reimbursement | insurer pays the hospital / you pay and claim later |
| network hospital / garage | partner hospital or garage for cashless claims |
| no-claim bonus (NCB) | discount or extra cover for years without claims |
| claim settlement / rejection / grievance | outcome of a claim / complaint |

## Questions to ask before buying

=== "Health"

    - **What is the waiting period** for pre-existing diseases?
    - **Is there a room-rent limit** or co-payment?
    - **Which hospitals near me** are in the cashless network?
    - **Are maternity / OPD / day-care treatments** covered?
    - **What are the main exclusions?**
    - **Is the premium likely to increase** with age?

=== "Life (term)"

    - **How much cover** do you recommend for my income and loans?
    - **Until what age** does the cover last?
    - **What happens if I miss** a premium payment?
    - **What is the claim settlement ratio** of the insurer?

=== "Motor & travel"

    - **Is zero depreciation** included? Is **roadside assistance** included?
    - **How does the no-claim bonus** work if I make a small claim?
    - (Travel) **Does it cover** medical emergencies, flight delays and lost baggage?
    - (Travel) **Is it accepted** for my visa application?

## Conversation 1: buying health insurance

```dialogue
Agent: Hello, this is Rohan from SafeLife Insurance. You requested a callback about health insurance.
You: Yes, thanks. I'm looking for a family floater for me, my wife and my son.
Agent: Sure. What cover are you looking for?
You: About ₹10 lakh. Before we talk about the price, could you tell me the waiting period for pre-existing diseases?
Agent: Three years for pre-existing diseases, and 30 days for new illnesses, except accidents.
You: My wife has thyroid. Will that be covered after three years?
Agent: Yes, if you declare it now.
You: Is there a room-rent limit or a co-payment?
Agent: No room-rent limit, and no co-payment below age 60.
You: Which hospitals near Whitefield are cashless?
Agent: I'll email you the list. The premium is about ₹21,000 a year.
You: Could you also email the policy wording and the list of exclusions? I'd like to read them before deciding.
Agent: Sure, I'll send everything today.
```

## Conversation 2: following up on a delayed claim

```dialogue
Agent: Thank you for calling. How can I help you?
You: Hi. I submitted a reimbursement claim three weeks ago, and there's no update. The claim number is CLM-448190.
Agent: Let me check… It's pending because the discharge summary is missing.
You: I uploaded it on the 5th. Could you check again, please?
Agent: You're right, I can see it now. It wasn't linked to the claim. I'll link it.
You: Thank you. How long will the settlement take now?
Agent: Within seven working days.
You: Could you send me an email confirming that the documents are complete?
Agent: Yes, I'll send it now.
You: And if it's rejected, what's the grievance process?
Agent: You can write to our grievance team. The details are in the email.
You: Thank you, Priya. That's very helpful.
```

## When things go wrong

- "**My claim was rejected** as a pre-existing disease, but I declared it when I bought the policy. I'd like it reviewed."
- "**The hospital says** the cashless approval hasn't come yet. Could you check?"
- "**My premium has gone up by 40%.** Could you explain why?"
- "**I'd like to port** my policy to another insurer. What's the process?"
