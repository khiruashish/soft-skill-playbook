# Restaurant & Café

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Ordering food is a good confidence workout:** short, friendly and low-risk. Team dinners and client
lunches use the same phrases.
</div>

## Arriving

- "**A table for two**, please."
- "We have a **reservation** under Gupta, 8 pm."
- "Could we sit **by the window / outside / somewhere quieter**?"
- "How long is the **wait**?"

## Ordering

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| Need time | "Could we have **a few more minutes**, please?" |
| Ask for a recommendation | "**What do you recommend?** What's popular here?" |
| Dietary needs | "**I'm vegetarian / vegan / allergic to peanuts.** Does this dish contain any?" |
| Customise | "Could I have this **less spicy / without onions / sauce on the side**?" |
| Ordering | "**I'll have** the paneer tikka, and **could I get** a lime soda?" |
| Portion | "**Is this enough for two**, or should we order more?" |
| Coffee | "**A medium cappuccino** with oat milk, please. **For here / to go.**" |

## During the meal

- "Excuse me, could we get **some more water**, please?"
- "Sorry, **this isn't what I ordered**. I asked for the grilled fish."
- "The food is **a bit cold**. Could you warm it up, please?"
- "Everything was **delicious**, thank you!"

## The bill

- "Could we have **the bill / the check**, please?"
- "Can we **split the bill**?" / "**I'll get this one.**"
- "Do you **accept cards / UPI**?"
- "Is **service charge included**?"

!!! tip "Hosting a client / team dinner"
    Let guests order first. Suggest 2–3 dishes if they're unsure. Avoid heavy work talk until the
    food arrives. Settle the bill quietly (tell the waiter in advance, or step away to pay).
