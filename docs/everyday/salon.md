## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| trim / cut / short / medium / long | how much to cut |
| fade / taper / layers / fringe | hair styles |
| sides / back / top / sideburns / neckline | parts of the head |
| beard trim / shave / shape | beard services |
| shampoo / conditioner / hair spa / head massage | treatments |
| hair colour / highlights / henna | colouring |
| facial / manicure / pedicure / waxing / threading | other services |
| appointment / walk-in | booked / without booking |

## Saying what you want

- "**Just a trim**, please. About **one centimetre** off."
- "**Short on the sides and back**, a bit **longer on top**."
- "**Not too short**, please."
- "**Keep the length**, just **tidy it up**."
- "Could you **trim my beard** and **shape** it? **Keep it short**."
- "I'd like **something like this**." (show a photo)

## Feedback during the service

- "**A little shorter** on the sides, please."
- "**That's perfect**, thank you."
- "**Please don't use** the machine on the top. Scissors only."
- "**The water is too hot.**"
- "**No gel / no products**, please."

## Questions you'll ask

- **Do I need an appointment**, or can I walk in?
- **How long is the wait?**
- **How much is** a haircut and beard trim?
- **Which shampoo do you recommend** for dandruff?
- **How often should I get** a haircut with this style?

## Conversation 1: at the barber

```dialogue
Barber: Please sit. What would you like today?
You: A haircut and a beard trim, please.
Barber: How would you like the haircut?
You: Short on the sides and back, and a bit longer on top. Not too short.
Barber: With a machine on the sides?
You: Yes, number 3 on the sides, and scissors on top.
(Later)
Barber: How's this?
You: Could you take a little more off the back? And make the sideburns a bit shorter.
Barber: Sure. And the beard?
You: Just tidy it up and keep the shape. Keep it short on the cheeks.
Barber: Done. Would you like a head massage?
You: How much is it?
Barber: ₹150 extra.
You: Okay, go ahead!
```

## Conversation 2: booking at a salon by phone

```dialogue
Receptionist: Good afternoon, Glow Salon.
You: Hi, I'd like to book a haircut and a hair spa for my wife on Saturday afternoon.
Receptionist: We have a slot at 3:30. Is that okay?
You: Yes, that works. How much are both services together?
Receptionist: ₹1,800 for both. We have a weekend combo offer.
You: Great. The booking is under Priya Gupta. Do you need a phone number?
Receptionist: Yes, please.
You: 98450 12345.
Receptionist: Done. Please come ten minutes early.
```
