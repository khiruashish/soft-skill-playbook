# Cab & Directions

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Confirm, clarify, stay safe.** Short, clear phrases work best with drivers and strangers.
</div>

## Booking / starting a ride

- "Hi, **are you here for Ashish**? Going to **Terminal 2**?" (confirm the driver before you get in)
- "Can you **follow the map**, please?"
- "**How long** will it take with this traffic?"
- "Could you **turn the AC up / down**, please?"
- "Could you **stop here**, please? Just after the signal."
- "Could you **wait 5 minutes**? I need to pick up a bag."

## Taxi without an app (fixed fare)

- "**How much** to the railway station?"
- "**Do you use the meter?**"
- "That seems high. **Could you do ₹300?**"

## Asking for directions

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"Excuse me, sorry to bother you. **How do I get to the Central Library?** Is it **walking distance**?"
</div>

| Ask | Useful when |
|---|---|
| "Is it **far from here**?" | Deciding walk vs cab |
| "**Which way** is the metro station?" | Need a direction |
| "Is there a **bus** that goes there?" | Public transport |
| "Can you **show me on the map**?" | Directions are complicated |
| "So, **straight, then left at the bank**. Right?" | Confirming (repeat back!) |

**Direction vocabulary:** go straight · turn left/right · take the second left · at the
signal/roundabout · opposite · next to · around the corner · cross the road.

## Safety phrases

- "Please **don't take a different route** without asking me."
- "I'm **sharing my live location** with my family."
- "Please **stop the car**. I'd like to get out here."

## Practice conversations

### Talking to the cab driver

```dialogue
Driver: Hello, Ashish sir?
You: Yes. Are you going to Terminal 2, Kempegowda Airport?
Driver: Yes, please sit.
You: Could you follow the map, please? There's usually traffic at Hebbal.
Driver: Okay. Should I take the toll road?
You: Yes, that's fine. I'll pay the toll. How long will it take?
Driver: About 55 minutes.
You: My flight is at 9:30. Could you please drive a bit faster, but safely?
Driver: Don't worry, we'll reach by 8:15.
You: Thank you. Could you turn the AC down a little?
```

### Asking a stranger for directions

```dialogue
You: Excuse me, sorry to bother you. Do you know where the City Library is?
Stranger: Yes, it's not far. Go straight for about 200 metres.
You: Okay, straight.
Stranger: At the traffic signal, turn left. It's the second building on your right, opposite the bank.
You: So, straight, left at the signal, and it's on the right, opposite the bank?
Stranger: Exactly. It's about five minutes on foot.
You: Thank you so much!
Stranger: You're welcome.
```
