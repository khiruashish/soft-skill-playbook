# Invitations & Celebrations

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** invite people, accept or decline, give wishes for birthdays, weddings and festivals,
and make conversation at celebrations.
</div>

## Inviting

- "**We're having** a small get-together on Saturday evening. **Would you like to come?**"
- "**It's my daughter's birthday** on the 12th. **We'd love it if you could join us.**"
- "**You're invited** to our housewarming on Sunday, 1 pm onwards."
- "**Are you free** for dinner next Friday?"

## Accepting & declining

| Accept | Decline politely |
|---|---|
| I'd love to! What time? | Thank you so much for inviting me. Unfortunately, I'm travelling that weekend. |
| Sounds great. Can I bring anything? | I'm so sorry, I have a prior commitment. Let's catch up soon! |
| Count me in! | I'd love to, but I can't make it this time. Have a wonderful evening! |
| Thank you, we'll definitely be there. | I might be a little late. Is that okay? |

## Wishes for every occasion

| Occasion | What to say |
|---|---|
| Birthday | Happy birthday! Wishing you a wonderful year ahead. |
| Wedding | Congratulations! Wishing you both a lifetime of happiness. |
| Anniversary | Happy anniversary! Here's to many more years together. |
| New baby | Congratulations on the little one! How are mother and baby doing? |
| New job / promotion | Congratulations! You really deserve it. |
| Festival | Happy Diwali! / Eid Mubarak! / Merry Christmas! / Happy Pongal! Wishing you and your family joy and prosperity. |
| Housewarming | Congratulations on the new home! It looks beautiful. |
| Condolence | I'm so sorry for your loss. Please let me know if there's anything I can do. |
| Get well | Get well soon! Take care and rest well. |

## Conversation 1: inviting a colleague

```dialogue
You: Hey Meena, are you free on Sunday evening?
Meena: I think so. Why?
You: We're having a housewarming party at our new flat. We'd love it if you could come.
Meena: Congratulations on the new place! I'd love to. What time?
You: Anytime after 6. I'll send you the location.
Meena: Great. Can I bring anything?
You: Just yourself! And your husband, of course.
Meena: Thank you. We'll be there.
```

## Conversation 2: at a wedding reception

```dialogue
You: Congratulations, Rahul! You look great. And congratulations, Divya!
Rahul: Thank you so much for coming! Divya, this is Ashish, my colleague from Bengaluru.
Divya: Nice to meet you! Thank you for travelling all this way.
You: It's a pleasure. The decorations are beautiful. Wishing you both a lifetime of happiness.
Divya: Thank you! Please have dinner before you leave.
You: Definitely. Rahul, the photos from the sangeet looked amazing.
Rahul: You should have come! Next time, no excuses.
You: Ha, I promise. Enjoy your evening!
```

## Small talk at parties

- "**How do you know** the host?"
- "**The food is amazing.** Have you tried the desserts?"
- "**Did you travel far** to get here?"
- "**It was lovely meeting you.** I hope to see you again soon."
