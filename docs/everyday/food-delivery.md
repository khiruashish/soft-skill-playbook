# Food Delivery & Takeaway

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** order food by phone, talk to delivery partners, and solve problems (late, wrong or
missing items) with app support.
</div>

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| takeaway / parcel / to go | food you take with you |
| delivery partner / rider | person who brings the order |
| order ID | the number of your order |
| ETA | expected time of arrival |
| missing item / wrong item | not in the bag / not what you ordered |
| spill / leaked | food came out of the box |
| refund / replacement / coupon | money back / new item / discount |
| contactless delivery | leave at the door |

## Questions you'll ask

- **Do you deliver to** HSR Layout?
- **How long will it take?**
- **Is there a minimum order** for delivery?
- **Can you make it less spicy?**
- **Can I pick it up** in 20 minutes?
- (To rider) **Where are you now?** / **Could you leave it at the gate**, please?
- (To support) **When will I get the refund?**

## Conversation 1: ordering takeaway by phone

```dialogue
Restaurant: Hello, Udupi Kitchen.
You: Hi, I'd like to place a takeaway order, please.
Restaurant: Sure, go ahead.
You: Two masala dosas, one plate of idli, and two filter coffees.
Restaurant: Anything else?
You: Can you make one dosa without onions?
Restaurant: Yes, no problem. That's ₹390. It'll be ready in 20 minutes.
You: Great. The name is Ashish. Can I pay when I pick it up?
Restaurant: Yes, cash or UPI.
You: Perfect. See you in 20 minutes.
```

## Conversation 2: missing item, app support chat

```dialogue
Support: Hi, I'm Neha from support. How can I help you?
You: Hi Neha. My order just arrived, but the paneer biryani is missing. The order ID is 48213.
Support: I'm sorry about that. Let me check. Could you share a photo of the items you received?
You: Sure, I've uploaded it.
Support: Thank you. I can see the biryani was not packed. Would you like a refund or a replacement?
You: A refund, please. It's getting late.
Support: Done. ₹280 will be refunded to your original payment method in 3 to 5 working days.
You: Can it go to my wallet instead? That would be faster.
Support: Yes, I've changed it. It's in your wallet now.
You: Thank you, Neha. That was quick.
```

## Talking to the delivery partner

- "Hi, I'm in **Block C, flat 402**. The **lift is on the left**."
- "**Please call when you reach the gate**; the security will let you in."
- "Could you **leave it with the security guard**? I'm in a meeting."
- "**Take your time**, drive safely."

## When things go wrong

- "The food is **cold / spilled / not sealed**."
- "This **isn't what I ordered**. I ordered veg, this is chicken."
- "It's **been over an hour**, and the order still says *preparing*."
- "I was **charged twice**."
