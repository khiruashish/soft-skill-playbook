# Movies & Weekend Plans

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** make plans with friends, book tickets, talk about films and shows, and give opinions
and recommendations.
</div>

## Making plans

| Suggest | Respond |
|---|---|
| **Do you want to** watch a movie this weekend? | Sure! Which one? |
| **How about** Saturday evening? | Saturday works for me. / Sunday is better for me. |
| **Shall we** go for the 7 pm show? | Perfect. |
| **Let's** meet at the mall at 6:30. | See you there! |
| **Are you up for** a trek on Sunday? | I'd love to, but I'm a bit tired. Maybe next week? |

## Booking tickets

- "**Two tickets** for the 7:15 show, please. **Recliner** seats if available."
- "**Are there any seats** in the middle row?"
- "**Is there a discount** on weekdays?"
- "**Can I cancel** the booking if plans change?"

## Talking about films and shows

| Ask | Answer |
|---|---|
| **What kind of movies do you like?** | I like thrillers and comedies. Not horror! |
| **What's it about?** | It's about a cricketer who makes a comeback after an injury. |
| **Who's in it?** | It stars… and it's directed by… |
| **How was it?** | It was brilliant / okay / a bit slow / too long. |
| **Would you recommend it?** | Definitely, especially the second half. / Not really, wait for it on OTT. |
| **Have you watched…?** | Yes, I loved it! / No, is it good? |

**Opinion words:** gripping, hilarious, touching, predictable, slow, overrated, a must-watch,
**no spoilers, please!**

## Conversation 1: making weekend plans

```dialogue
Friend: Any plans for the weekend?
You: Not yet. Do you want to watch the new Malayalam thriller? Everyone's talking about it.
Friend: Sure! Is it in theatres or on OTT?
You: In theatres. How about the 7 pm show on Saturday?
Friend: Saturday works. Should we have dinner after?
You: Good idea. There's a nice Chettinad place near the mall.
Friend: Perfect. Will you book the tickets?
You: Yes, I'll book two recliners and send you the details.
```

## Conversation 2: after the movie

```dialogue
Friend: So, what did you think?
You: I loved it! The first half was a bit slow, but the twist at the interval was brilliant.
Friend: I know! I didn't see it coming at all.
You: And the background music was amazing. Who was the composer?
Friend: Sushin Shyam, I think. What did you think of the ending?
You: Honestly, a little predictable. But overall, it's a must-watch.
Friend: Agreed. Should we watch the director's earlier film on Netflix next weekend?
You: Sure, let's do that!
```
