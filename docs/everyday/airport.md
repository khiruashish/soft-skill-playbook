# At the Airport

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**An airport is a series of short conversations with people who are busy.** Have your documents ready,
answer exactly what's asked, and don't be shy about asking for help.
</div>

## Your airport journey

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Check-in & bag drop</div>
<div>2. Security screening</div>
<div>3. Immigration / passport control <span>— international</span></div>
<div>4. Gate & boarding</div>
<div>5. On the plane</div>
<div>6. Arrival: immigration, baggage, customs</div>
</div>

## 1. Check-in counter

=== "What you'll be asked"

    | They ask | You answer |
    |---|---|
    | "May I see your passport / ID and ticket, please?" | "Sure, here you go." |
    | "Where are you flying to today?" | "To Singapore, then connecting to Sydney." |
    | "How many bags are you checking in?" | "Just one, and one cabin bag." |
    | "Did you pack your bags yourself?" | "Yes, I did." |
    | "Are you carrying any batteries, power banks, or liquids in your checked bag?" | "No, the power bank is in my cabin bag." |
    | "Window or aisle?" | "Aisle, please." |
    | "Your bag is 3 kg over. There's an excess charge." | "Could I move some items to my cabin bag instead?" |

=== "What you can ask"

    - "Could I get an **aisle / window** seat, please?"
    - "Is it possible to sit **together** with my colleague?"
    - "Is my bag **checked through** to my final destination, or do I collect it in transit?"
    - "What time does **boarding** start, and which **gate**?"
    - "Is there any **extra legroom / exit row** seat available?"
    - "What's the **baggage allowance** on this ticket?"
    - "Can I add my **frequent flyer number**?"
    - "Is the flight **on time**?"

## 2. Security

| You'll hear | Meaning / what to do |
|---|---|
| "Laptops and liquids out, please." | Put laptop & liquids (≤100 ml, in a clear bag) in a separate tray |
| "Anything in your pockets?" | Wallet, phone, keys, coins go in the tray |
| "Please remove your belt / shoes / jacket." | Do it, place in tray |
| "Step through, please. Arms up." | Walk through the scanner / body scanner |
| "Is this your bag? I need to check it." | "Yes, sure, go ahead." Stay calm. Random checks are normal. |

**You can ask:** "Do I need to take out my tablet / charger too?" · "Can I keep my shoes on?"

## 3. Immigration (international)

!!! info "Golden rule"
    Answer **only what's asked**, **honestly**, **briefly**. Keep documents ready: passport, visa,
    return ticket, hotel booking, invitation letter.

| Officer asks | Good answer |
|---|---|
| "What's the purpose of your visit?" | "Business. I'm attending a tech conference." / "Tourism." |
| "How long will you stay?" | "Five days. I return on the 12th." |
| "Where will you be staying?" | "At the Marina Hotel, here's the booking." |
| "Who is your employer / what do you do?" | "I'm a senior software engineer at XYZ Technologies." |
| "Have you been here before?" | "Yes, once in 2024." / "No, first time." |
| "Are you carrying more than [X] in cash?" | "No." / "Yes, here's my declaration." |
| "Do you have a return ticket?" | "Yes, here it is." |

**If you don't understand:** "Sorry, could you repeat that, please?" Don't guess.

## 4. At the gate & boarding

**You can ask:**

- "Excuse me, is this the gate for the **Mumbai flight**?"
- "Has **boarding started** for AI 302?"
- "Is the flight **delayed**? Do you know the new time?"
- "Which **boarding group** am I in?"
- "Can I take this bag **on board**, or does it need to be gate-checked?"

**Announcements decoded:**

| Announcement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "Pre-boarding for families and passengers needing assistance" | Not you yet (unless it applies) |
| "Now boarding zones 1 and 2" | Check your boarding pass zone/group |
| "Final call for passenger…" | Hurry! The gate is closing |
| "Gate change" | Check the screens, move to the new gate |

## 5. On the plane

- "Excuse me, I think you're in my seat. I'm **14C**." (show boarding pass, smile)
- "Could you help me put my bag in the **overhead bin**?"
- "Could I have **a glass of water**, please?"
- "Is it possible to **change seats**? There's an empty one at the back."
- "Excuse me, could I **get past**? Thank you."
- "What time do we **land** (local time)?"

## 6. Arrival

=== "Baggage"

    - "Which **belt** is the bag from flight SQ 421 on?"
    - "Where is the **baggage services / lost & found** desk?"

=== "Lost / damaged bag"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Hi, **my bag didn't arrive** on flight SQ 421 from Bangalore. Here's my baggage tag. It's a
    **large black suitcase with a red ribbon**. Could you file a report? **When can I expect it, and
    can it be delivered to my hotel?** Is there an allowance for essentials meanwhile?"
    </div>

    Always get a **reference number (PIR)** and a phone/email for follow-up.

=== "Customs"

    | Asked | Answer |
    |---|---|
    | "Anything to declare?" | "No, nothing." / "Yes, I have [item]." |
    | "Are you carrying food items?" | Be honest: "Yes, some packaged snacks." |

## When things go wrong

=== "Flight delayed / cancelled"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "My flight to Delhi has been **cancelled**. **What are my options?** Can you rebook me on the
    next available flight? Will the airline provide a **hotel / meal voucher**? And can I get that in
    writing?"
    </div>

=== "Missed connection"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "My first flight was delayed and **I missed my connection** to London. Here's my boarding pass.
    Can you put me on the next flight? **Will my checked bag be transferred too?**"
    </div>

=== "Can't find your gate / lost"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Excuse me, I'm a bit lost. **How do I get to the international transfers / Gate B12?** Is it a
    long walk, or should I take the train?"
    </div>

## Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Layover / Stopover | A stop between flights (short / long) |
| Transit / Transfer | Changing planes |
| Boarding pass | The pass that lets you board |
| Carry-on / Cabin bag | Bag you take on the plane |
| Checked baggage | Bag that goes in the hold |
| Excess baggage | Weight over the allowance (fee) |
| Departures / Arrivals | Leaving / coming areas |
| Duty-free | Tax-free shops after security |
| Jet lag | Tiredness from time-zone change |
| Red-eye | Overnight flight |

## Practice conversations

### Check-in with extra baggage

```dialogue
Staff: Good morning. Passport and ticket, please.
You: Good morning. Here you are. I'm flying to Dubai.
Staff: How many bags are you checking in?
You: Two bags.
Staff: Your allowance is 30 kilos. Together they're 34 kilos.
You: Oh. Can I move some things to my cabin bag?
Staff: Sure, you can use the side area.
(Five minutes later)
You: I've moved some clothes. Could you weigh them again?
Staff: 29.5 kilos. Perfect. Window or aisle?
You: Aisle, please. And could you add my frequent flyer number?
Staff: Done. Boarding is at 9:10 from gate 24.
You: Thank you!
```

### Flight delay at the gate

```dialogue
You: Excuse me, the board says the Delhi flight is delayed. Do you know the new time?
Staff: Yes, it's now 4:30 instead of 2:45. There's a technical issue.
You: I have a connecting flight in Delhi at 7. Will I make it?
Staff: It will be tight. Let me check if we can protect you on the 9 pm connection.
You: Thank you. Will my bag also be moved?
Staff: Yes, it's checked through. Here's a meal voucher for the delay.
You: Thanks. Could you write the new connection details on my boarding pass?
```
