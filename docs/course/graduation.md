## Part 1: English basics (Days 1–13)

**Make questions** for the answers:

1. ______? → I've worked here for six years.
2. ______? → The train leaves from platform 4.
3. ______? → No, I didn't get the email.
4. ______? → It costs ₹1,200.

**Fix the mistakes:**

5. *Myself Ravi, I am working in Infosys since 2019.*
6. *Did you went to office yesterday?*
7. *Please do the needful and revert back.*
8. *If it will rain, we cancel the trip.*

??? success "Answers"
    1. How long have you worked here? 2. Which platform does the train leave from? 3. Did you get my email?
    4. How much does it cost? 5. I'm Ravi. I've been working at Infosys since 2019.
    6. Did you go to the office yesterday? 7. Could you please [specific action] and reply?
    8. If it rains, we'll cancel the trip.

## Part 2: Communication (Days 14–18)

9. Rewrite with the point first: *"So I was checking logs and then I saw the cache was empty and then… so the release will be one day late."*
10. Write two clarifying questions for: *"Make the dashboard better."*
11. Replace the filler: *"Basically, actually, I think maybe we could, like, try caching."*

??? success "Sample answers"
    9. "The release will be one day late. The cache was empty, so we need to re-run the tests."
    10. "Which part of the dashboard matters most to you?" / "What does 'better' mean: faster, clearer, or more data?"
    11. "I suggest we try caching."

## Part 3: Everyday conversations (Days 19–48)

Say each of these out loud in one or two sentences:

12. At a railway counter, ask for a ticket and which platform.
13. Tell a doctor about a symptom: where, what kind, since when.
14. Complain politely to a neighbour about noise.
15. Report a missing item in a food delivery, with your order ID.
16. Call an ambulance: what happened, exact location, landmark.
17. Tell a gym trainer your goal and one health limitation.
18. Ask a landlord about deposit, notice period and repairs.

??? success "Sample answers"
    12. "One ticket to Mysuru on the 6:15 train, please. Which platform does it leave from?"
    13. "I've had a sharp pain in my lower back for three days. It's worse in the morning."
    14. "Hi, sorry to bother you. The music is quite loud in our flat after 11. Could you turn it down a little at night?"
    15. "My order 48213 arrived without the paneer biryani. Could you refund it, please?"
    16. "A man has collapsed and isn't breathing properly, at 12 MG Road, opposite the Metro station. Please send an ambulance."
    17. "I want to lose eight kilos. I have some lower back pain, so I need to avoid heavy lifting at first."
    18. "How much is the deposit, and is it fully refundable? What's the notice period, and who pays for repairs?"

## Part 4 & 5: Workplace and career (Days 49–74)

19. Give a 3-line stand-up update with a blocker.
20. Raise a risk to your manager with two options and a recommendation.
21. Explain a technical issue to a non-technical person with one analogy.
22. Say no to extra work politely, with an alternative.
23. Answer "Tell me about yourself" in under 60 seconds.
24. Respond to a salary offer that's lower than you expected.

!!! tip "Score yourself"
    For each item, give yourself **2** (easy, confident), **1** (I could do it, slowly) or **0** (I got
    stuck). **40+** is excellent. **25–39**: re-do the days where you scored 0. **Under 25**: repeat
    Phase 1 and the everyday days you'll need soonest. There's no shame in a second round.
