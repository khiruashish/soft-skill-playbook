# Pronunciation & Accent

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** be **clearly understood**. You don't need to lose your accent. Clear pronunciation,
the right word stress and a steady pace matter far more than sounding British or American.
</div>

## What actually matters

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Pace <span>— slow down; most "accent" problems are speed problems</span></div>
<div>2. Word stress <span>— stress the right syllable: DE-ve-lop-er, de-VE-lop</span></div>
<div>3. Clear consonants <span>— finish your words: "asked", "world", "first"</span></div>
<div>4. Sentence stress <span>— stress the important words: "The release is on FRIDAY"</span></div>
<div>5. Specific sounds <span>— v/w, th, and vowel length</span></div>
</div>

| Word | Stress | | Word | Stress |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| developer | de**VEL**oper | | company | **COM**pany |
| develop | de**VEL**op | | hotel | ho**TEL** |
| analysis | a**NAL**ysis | | analyst | **AN**alyst |
| architecture | **AR**chitecture | | process | **PRO**cess |
| determine | de**TER**mine | | photograph | **PHO**tograph |
| photography | pho**TOG**raphy | | category | **CAT**egory |
| comfortable | **COMF**-ter-ble (3 syllables) | | vegetable | **VEJ**-ta-ble (3 syllables) |

## Sounds to practise

=== "v and w"

    - **v**: top teeth touch the bottom lip → **v**ery, **v**ideo, **v**ersion, ser**v**er
    - **w**: round your lips, no teeth → **w**est, **w**ork, **w**indow, soft**w**are
    - Practise: "**W**e **w**ill **v**erify the ne**w** **v**ersion on **W**ednesday."

=== "th"

    - **th** (soft): tongue between teeth, blow air → **th**ink, **th**ree, **th**anks, ma**th**
    - **th** (voiced): same, with voice → **th**is, **th**at, **th**ey, o**th**er
    - Not "tink" or "dis". Practise: "**Th**ank you, I **th**ink **th**at's **th**e **th**ird **th**ing."

=== "s + consonant"

    - Don't add a vowel before **s**: "**s**chool" not "i-school", "**s**tation" not "i-station",
      "**s**pecial" not "i-special", "**s**tart" not "i-start".

=== "Word endings"

    - Pronounce final sounds: "as**ked**" (askt), "wor**ld**", "fir**st**", "tex**ts**", "mon**ths**".
    - -ed endings: work**ed** (t), play**ed** (d), wait**ed** (id).

## Sentence stress and pausing

Stress the words that carry meaning (nouns, main verbs, numbers, "not"):

- "The **release** is **NOT** on **Friday**, it's on **MONDAY**."
- "We **reduced** latency by **SIXTY** percent."

Pause at commas and before important points: "The root cause | was a **timeout**."

## Easy ways to improve

1. **Shadowing:** play a 30-second clip of a clear speaker. Repeat each sentence right after them,
   copying the rhythm.
2. **Record & compare:** read a paragraph, record it, compare with the original.
3. **Dictionary audio:** check the stress of any new word (online dictionaries have audio).
4. **Slow down by 20%** in meetings. Clear beats fast.
5. **Minimal pairs:** practise pairs that differ by one sound: vest/west, three/tree, ship/sheep, full/fool.

## Conversation: when someone doesn't understand you

```dialogue
Colleague: Sorry, could you repeat that? I didn't catch it.
You: Sure. I said we should verify the new version on Wednesday.
Colleague: Verify the… sorry, version?
You: The new VERSION of the app. Version two point three.
Colleague: Ah, got it! Wednesday works for me.
You: Great. I'll also send it in the chat, so we have it in writing.
Colleague: Perfect, thanks.
```

!!! tip "Rephrase, don't repeat louder"
    If someone doesn't understand, **say it more slowly**, **use a simpler word**, or **type it in chat**.
    Repeating the same sentence faster or louder rarely helps.
