# Describing People, Places & Problems

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** describe people, places, things and problems clearly, and compare options. You'll use
this to report lost items, explain a bug, choose a hotel, or find a person in a crowd.
</div>

## Adjectives: where they go

<div class="ssp-formula">a / an + <em>adjective</em> + noun · be + <em>adjective</em></div>

- a **big black** suitcase · an **old** building · a **quiet** room
- The room is **quiet**. · The app is **slow**. · She looks **tired**.

**Order (when you use several):** opinion → size → age → colour → material → noun:
"a **nice small old red leather** bag" (in practice, use 2–3).

## Describing people

| Topic | Words |
|---|---|
| Height & build | tall, short, average height, slim, well-built |
| Hair | short / long, straight / curly, black / grey, bald |
| Age | young, middle-aged, elderly, in his thirties |
| Clothes | wearing a blue shirt, a red saree, glasses, a cap |
| Personality | friendly, helpful, quiet, confident, patient, strict |

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"He's **about 60, short, with grey hair and glasses**. He's **wearing a white kurta**."
</div>

## Describing places and things

- **Place:** crowded, quiet, clean, noisy, modern, spacious, well-located, far from / close to…
- **Things:** heavy, light, broken, new, second-hand, waterproof, made of steel / plastic / cotton
- **Location words:** next to, opposite, behind, in front of, between, near, on the corner of

## Comparing

| Short adjectives | Long adjectives | Irregular |
|---|---|---|
| cheap → cheap**er** → the cheap**est** | expensive → **more** expensive → the **most** expensive | good → better → the best |
| big → bigg**er** → the bigg**est** | comfortable → **more** comfortable | bad → worse → the worst |
| easy → eas**ier** → the eas**iest** | convenient → **more** convenient | far → farther → the farthest |

- The metro is **faster than** the bus. · This is **the cheapest** option. · It's **not as** big **as** the other room.

## Describing a problem (any kind)

<div class="ssp-formula">What's wrong → <em>since when</em> → how bad → what you tried</div>

| Situation | Description |
|---|---|
| Lost bag | "It's a **medium-sized black backpack** with a **red keychain**. It has my **laptop** inside." |
| Broken appliance | "The washing machine **makes a loud noise** and **doesn't drain**. It **started yesterday**." |
| App bug | "The app **freezes** on the payment screen **every time** I tap 'Pay'. I've **restarted** the phone." |
| Health | "I've had a **sharp pain** in my lower back **for three days**. It's **worse** in the morning." |

**Useful problem words:** broken, not working, stuck, leaking, cracked, missing, damaged, too loud,
too slow, freezing, overheating, keeps (+ -ing): "It **keeps crashing**."

## Conversation: lost bag at the metro station

```dialogue
You: Excuse me, I think I left my bag on the train.
Staff: Which train, and what time?
You: The one that arrived at platform 2 at about 9:15, from Whitefield.
Staff: Can you describe the bag?
You: It's a medium-sized black backpack. It's quite heavy, and it has a red keychain on the zip.
Staff: What's inside?
You: My laptop, a charger, and a blue water bottle. My office ID card is in the front pocket.
Staff: Okay. Please fill in this form. We'll check with the last station.
You: Thank you. How long does it usually take?
Staff: About an hour. Please wait near the customer care counter.
You: Okay. Here's my phone number, in case you find it.
```
