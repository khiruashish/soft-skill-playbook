## Past simple: finished actions

<div class="ssp-formula">Regular: verb + <em>-ed</em> · Irregular: learn the form · Questions & negatives: <em>did</em> + base verb</div>

| Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|
| I **called** the bank. | I **didn't call** the bank. | **Did** you **call** the bank? |
| She **went** to Delhi. | She **didn't go** to Delhi. | Where **did** she **go**? |
| The app **crashed**. | The app **didn't crash**. | Why **did** the app **crash**? |
| It **was** late. / They **were** late. | It **wasn't** late. | **Was** it late? |

## Telling a story in order

Use sequence words: **First… · Then… · After that… · Suddenly… · Finally…**

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"**Yesterday** I **left** home at 8. **First**, I **took** an auto to the station. **Then** I **found**
that my train **was** two hours late. **So** I **called** my manager and **told** her. **Finally**, I
**worked** from the station café and **joined** the 11 o'clock meeting on my phone."
</div>

## Present perfect: experience and recent results

<div class="ssp-formula">have / has + <em>past participle</em> (been, done, seen, gone, eaten…)</div>

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Life experience (no time given) | **Have** you ever **been** to Kerala? · I**'ve** never **flown** business class. |
| Recent result that matters now | I**'ve lost** my wallet. (I don't have it now) · The flight **has landed**. |
| Up to now (with for / since) | I**'ve worked** here **for** five years / **since** 2021. |

!!! warning "Past simple vs present perfect"
    With a finished time (yesterday, last year, in 2020), use **past simple**:
    ❌ *I have seen him yesterday.* → ✅ **I saw him yesterday.**

## Conversation: reporting a problem at the hotel desk

```dialogue
You: Good morning. I had a problem in my room last night.
Receptionist: I'm sorry to hear that. What happened?
You: The AC stopped working at around midnight. I called the front desk, but nobody answered.
Receptionist: I apologise. Did anyone come to check it?
You: No, nobody came. I didn't sleep well because it was very hot.
Receptionist: I'm really sorry. Have you checked out yet?
You: Not yet. I'm leaving at 11.
Receptionist: I've informed the manager. We'll give you a 20% discount on last night's stay.
You: Thank you, I appreciate that.
```
