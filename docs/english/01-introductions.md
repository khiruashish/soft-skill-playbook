# Greetings & Introducing Yourself

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** greet anyone politely, introduce yourself in 30 seconds, and ask the first questions
that start a conversation.
</div>

## Greetings: which one to use

| Situation | Greeting | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| Morning (until 12 pm) | Good morning! | Good morning! How are you? |
| Afternoon (12–5 pm) | Good afternoon! | Good afternoon! |
| Evening (after 5 pm) | Good evening! | Good evening! |
| Friendly, any time | Hi! / Hello! / Hey! (friends only) | Hi! How's it going? |
| First meeting | Nice to meet you. | Nice to meet you too. |
| Leaving | Bye! / See you tomorrow! / Have a good day! | You too! / Bye! Take care. |

!!! warning "\"Good night\" is only for leaving"
    "Good night" means goodbye at night, or before sleep. When you **arrive** in the evening, say
    **"Good evening"**.

## "How are you?" and good answers

| Question | Natural answers |
|---|---|
| How are you? | I'm good, thanks. And you? / Not bad, thank you. How about you? |
| How's it going? | Pretty good! / Going well, thanks. |
| How was your weekend? | It was great, thanks. I visited my parents. |
| What's up? (casual) | Not much! / Just working. You? |

Always **ask back**: "And you?" or "How about you?" It keeps the conversation going.

## Introduce yourself: the 4-line formula

<div class="ssp-formula">Name → <em>Work / Study</em> → Where from / live → One personal detail</div>

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"Hi, **I'm Ashish.** **I'm a software developer** at a product company. **I'm from Lucknow**, but I
live in Bengaluru now. **In my free time, I like** cooking and watching cricket."
</div>

| Part | Sentence starters |
|---|---|
| Name | I'm … / My name is … / You can call me … |
| Work | I work as a … / I work at … / I'm a … / I'm studying … |
| Place | I'm from … / I grew up in … / I live in … / I've lived in … for 5 years |
| Personal | I like … / I enjoy … / In my free time, I … / I'm interested in … |

## First questions to ask someone

- What's your name? / Sorry, what was your name again?
- Where are you from?
- What do you do? (= What is your job?)
- How long have you been here / in this company?
- Do you live nearby?
- What do you like to do on weekends?

## Conversation 1: meeting a new colleague

```dialogue
Priya: Hi! I don't think we've met. I'm Priya.
You: Hi Priya, nice to meet you. I'm Ashish. I joined the backend team on Monday.
Priya: Oh, welcome! Nice to meet you too. Where were you before this?
You: I was at a fintech company in Pune for six years.
Priya: That's great. Are you from Pune originally?
You: No, I'm from Lucknow. But I've lived in Pune and now Bengaluru. How about you?
Priya: I'm from Kochi. I've been here for three years. I'm on the mobile team.
You: Nice! I'd love to learn how the mobile app works. Maybe we can have lunch sometime?
Priya: Sure, let's do that this week.
You: Great. See you around, Priya!
```

## Conversation 2: meeting a neighbour

```dialogue
Neighbour: Hello! Are you the new family in 304?
You: Yes, hello! We moved in last Saturday. I'm Ashish.
Neighbour: Welcome! I'm Mr. Sharma, from 302. How are you finding the building?
You: It's very nice, thank you. Everyone seems friendly. How long have you lived here?
Neighbour: Almost ten years now. If you need anything, just knock.
You: That's very kind of you. Thank you, Mr. Sharma. Have a good evening!
Neighbour: You too!
```

## Practice

**1. Choose the right greeting.**

1. It's 9 am. You meet your manager. → ______
2. It's 7 pm. You arrive at a party. → ______
3. It's 10 pm. You leave a friend's house. → ______

??? success "Answers"
    1. Good morning! 2. Good evening! 3. Good night! (or "Bye, have a good night!")

**2. Write your 4-line introduction** using the formula above, then say it out loud 3 times. Time
yourself: it should take about 20–30 seconds.

**3. Make the question.** Write a question for each answer.

1. ______? → I'm from Jaipur.
2. ______? → I'm a data analyst.
3. ______? → I've been here for two years.

??? success "Answers"
    1. Where are you from? 2. What do you do? 3. How long have you been here?
