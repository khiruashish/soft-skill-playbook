# Numbers, Time, Dates & Money

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** say and understand prices, phone numbers, times, dates and spellings without
confusion. You need these at every counter, on every call, and at every station.
</div>

## Big numbers

| Number | Say it |
|---|---|
| 150 | one hundred **and** fifty |
| 1,250 | one thousand two hundred and fifty |
| 15,000 | fifteen thousand |
| 1,00,000 (Indian) = 100,000 | one lakh = one hundred thousand |
| 10,00,000 = 1,000,000 | ten lakh = one million |
| 1,00,00,000 = 10,000,000 | one crore = ten million |

!!! tip "With international colleagues, convert lakh and crore"
    "₹25 lakh" → **"about 2.5 million rupees"** or **"around 30,000 US dollars"**.

**Teens vs tens:** say them clearly. thir**TEEN** (13) vs **THIR**ty (30), fif**TEEN** (15) vs **FIF**ty (50).
If unsure, confirm: "One-five or five-zero?"

## Money

- ₹250 → "two hundred and fifty rupees"
- ₹99.50 → "ninety-nine rupees fifty (paise)"
- $4.99 → "four ninety-nine" or "four dollars ninety-nine cents"
- **How much is it?** · **How much does it cost?** · **What's the total?** · **Is that including tax?**
- "That **comes to** ₹840." · "Here's **₹1,000**." · "Your **change** is ₹160."

## Phone numbers & codes

Say digits one by one, in groups: **98450 12345** → "nine-eight-four-five-oh, one-two-three-four-five".
**0** = "zero" or "oh" · **44** = "double four" · **777** = "triple seven".
Always **read numbers back**: "Let me repeat that: nine-eight-four…"

## Time

| Time | Say it |
|---|---|
| 7:00 | seven o'clock |
| 7:15 | seven fifteen / quarter past seven |
| 7:30 | seven thirty / half past seven |
| 7:45 | seven forty-five / quarter to eight |
| 7:10 | seven ten / ten past seven |
| 7:50 | seven fifty / ten to eight |
| 19:00 | 7 pm / seven in the evening |

**at** 6 pm · **in** the morning / afternoon / evening · **at** night · **around** 7 (approximately) ·
**sharp** (exactly: "10 o'clock sharp")

## Dates

- Write: 15 March 2026 / 15/03/2026 (India, UK) · March 15, 2026 / 03/15/2026 (US). In global teams, write the month as a word.
- Say: "**the fifteenth of March**" or "**March fifteenth**".
- Ordinals: 1st first · 2nd second · 3rd third · 4th fourth · 5th fifth · 21st twenty-first · 22nd twenty-second · 31st thirty-first
- **on** Monday / on 5 June · **in** June / in 2026 · **at** the weekend (UK) / **on** the weekend (US)

## Spelling out loud

Use a word for each letter when the line is bad: **A** for Apple, **B** for Bombay, **S** for Sugar,
**M** for Mumbai, **N** for Nagpur, **P** for Pune, **D** for Delhi…

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"My name is Gupta: **G** for Goa, **U** for Umbrella, **P** for Pune, **T** for Tiger, **A** for Apple."
</div>

## Conversation: booking a table on the phone

```dialogue
Restaurant: Good evening, Spice Garden. How can I help you?
You: Hi, I'd like to book a table for six people on Saturday, the 14th.
Restaurant: Sure. What time?
You: At 8 pm, please.
Restaurant: Let me check… Yes, that's available. Could I have your name?
You: Gupta. G for Goa, U for Umbrella, P for Pune, T for Tiger, A for Apple.
Restaurant: Thank you. And a phone number?
You: It's 98450 12345.
Restaurant: 98450 12345?
You: That's right.
Restaurant: Done. A table for six, Saturday the 14th, at 8 pm.
You: Perfect. Is there a minimum charge for groups?
Restaurant: No, there isn't.
You: Great, thank you. See you on Saturday!
```
