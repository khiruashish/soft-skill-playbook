## Two presents, two jobs

| | Present simple | Present continuous |
|---|---|---|
| Use for | habits, routines, facts | right now, these days, temporary |
| Form | I **work** · She **works** | I **am working** · She **is working** |
| Key words | always, usually, every day, on Mondays | now, right now, at the moment, today, this week |
| Example | I **take** the metro to work. | I'm **taking** an auto today because the metro is closed. |

## Present simple

<div class="ssp-formula">I / you / we / they + <em>verb</em> · he / she / it + <em>verb + s</em></div>

| Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|
| I start work at 9. | I don't start work at 9. | Do you start work at 9? |
| She lives in Chennai. | She doesn't live in Chennai. | Does she live in Chennai? |
| The shop closes at 10. | The shop doesn't close at 10. | When does the shop close? |

**Spelling:** work → work**s** · watch → watch**es** · go → go**es** · study → stud**ies** · have → **has**

**How often:** always (100%) → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never (0%).
Position: before the main verb (I **usually** walk), after *be* (I'm **always** late).

## Present continuous

<div class="ssp-formula">am / is / are + <em>verb-ing</em></div>

| Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|
| I'm working from home today. | I'm not working today. | Are you working today? |
| He's talking to the client. | He isn't talking to the client. | Who is he talking to? |
| They're building a new app. | They aren't building it. | What are they building? |

!!! warning "Some verbs are not used in -ing (state verbs)"
    know, understand, want, like, need, believe, own, belong, seem.
    ❌ *I am knowing him.* ❌ *I am having two kids.* ✅ **I know him.** ✅ **I have two kids.**

## Your daily routine: useful sentences

- I **wake up** at 6:30 and **go** for a walk.
- I **have** breakfast at 8 and **leave** for work at 8:45.
- I usually **take** the metro; it **takes** about 35 minutes.
- We **have** a stand-up meeting at 10.
- I **get back** home around 7 and **spend** time with my kids.
- On weekends, I **play** badminton and **visit** my parents.

## Conversation: catching up with a friend

```dialogue
Friend: Hey! What are you doing these days?
You: I'm working on a new project at work. We're building a delivery app.
Friend: Nice! Do you still work from home?
You: Not every day. I go to the office three days a week now.
Friend: How do you get there?
You: I usually take the metro. But this week I'm driving, because the metro line is closed for repairs.
Friend: And are you still playing badminton?
You: Yes, I play every Saturday morning. Do you want to join us?
Friend: Sure! What time do you start?
You: We start at seven. I'll send you the location.
```

**1. Simple or continuous?**

1. She ______ (work) at a bank. 2. Look! It ______ (rain). 3. I ______ (not like) long meetings.
4. We ______ (have) lunch now. Can I call you back? 5. The train usually ______ (leave) on time.

??? success "Answers"
    1. works 2. is raining 3. don't like 4. are having 5. leaves
