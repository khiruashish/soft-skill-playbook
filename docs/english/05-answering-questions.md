# Answering Questions

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** answer questions clearly and politely: short or full, saying "no" politely, giving
reasons, and saying "I'm not sure" without sounding weak.
</div>

## The answer ladder: choose your length

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Short <span>— "Yes, it is." · fast, casual, enough for simple facts</span></div>
<div>2. Short + detail <span>— "Yes, it's on the second floor." · the most useful everyday answer</span></div>
<div>3. Answer + reason <span>— "No, I can't come, because I have a client call." · for requests and decisions</span></div>
<div>4. Answer + reason + next step <span>— "…but I can join at 5. Does that work?" · the most professional answer</span></div>
</div>

## Answer the question that was asked

| Question | ❌ Unclear answer | ✅ Clear answer |
|---|---|---|
| When will it be ready? | "We are working on it." | "By Thursday evening." |
| How much is the room? | "It's a very good room, sir…" | "₹3,500 a night, including breakfast." |
| Where do you live? | "Near." | "In Indiranagar, near the metro station." |
| Did you finish the report? | "Actually I was doing many things…" | "Not yet. I'll send it by 4 pm." |

**Answer first, explain after.**

## Saying no politely

| Situation | Polite "no" |
|---|---|
| Offer | No, thank you. / I'm fine, thanks. |
| Request you can't do | I'm sorry, I can't today. Could we do it tomorrow? |
| Invitation | Thank you so much for inviting me. Unfortunately, I have plans that day. |
| Something not available | Sorry, we don't have that. We have this instead. |
| Disagreeing | I'm not sure I agree. Here's why… |

## When you don't know or aren't sure

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| "I don't know." (and stop) | "I'm not sure, but I'll find out and let you know." |
| A guess presented as fact | "I think it's on Friday, but let me confirm." |
| Silence | "Good question. Let me think for a second." |
| Pretending you understood | "Sorry, could you say that again?" |

## Answers with numbers, places and time

- **Time:** "At half past six." / "Around 7." / "In about twenty minutes." / "By Friday."
- **Place:** "On the second floor." / "Next to the bank." / "Opposite the bus stand."
- **Frequency:** "Every day." / "Twice a week." / "Once a month." / "Hardly ever."
- **Duration:** "For three years." / "Since 2021." / "About two hours."

## Conversation: the HR call

```dialogue
HR: Hi, is this Ashish? I'm calling from Brightsoft about the backend role.
You: Yes, this is Ashish. Thanks for calling.
HR: Is now a good time to talk for ten minutes?
You: Yes, that's fine.
HR: Great. How many years of experience do you have with Java?
You: About nine years. I've used Java with Spring Boot for most of that time.
HR: And what's your current notice period?
You: It's sixty days, but it may be negotiable.
HR: Are you open to working from the office three days a week?
You: Yes, I am. The office is about forty minutes from my home.
HR: What's your expected salary?
You: I'm not sure yet. I'd like to know more about the role first. Could you share the budgeted range?
HR: Sure, I'll email it to you today.
You: Thank you. I'll look out for it.
```

## Practice

**1. Give a short + detail answer.**

1. Is there an ATM near here? 2. Do you work from home? 3. Have you been to Goa?

??? success "Sample answers"
    1. Yes, there's one next to the pharmacy. 2. Sometimes. I work from home on Mondays and Fridays.
    3. Yes, I have. I went there last December.

**2. Say no politely.**

1. "Would you like some sweets?" (you're full) 2. "Can you work this Sunday?" (you can't)

??? success "Sample answers"
    1. No, thank you. They look delicious, but I'm full. 2. I'm sorry, I can't this Sunday. I could
    work an extra hour on Monday and Tuesday instead. Would that help?

**3. Rewrite the weak answer.** "When will the fix be deployed?" → "Actually we are trying, maybe soon."

??? success "Sample answer"
    "Today by 6 pm. We're running the final tests now."
