# Wh- Questions

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** ask for any information you need (where, when, how much, how long, why) with the
right word order.
</div>

## The question words

| Word | Asks about | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | thing, information | What is your name? / What time is it? |
| **Where** | place | Where is platform 5? |
| **When** | time | When does the shop open? |
| **Why** | reason | Why is the flight delayed? |
| **Who** | person | Who is the project manager? |
| **Which** | choice from a group | Which bus goes to the airport? |
| **Whose** | owner | Whose laptop is this? |
| **How** | way, method, condition | How do I get to the metro? / How are you? |
| **How much** | price, uncountable amount | How much is this? / How much water do you need? |
| **How many** | countable number | How many people are coming? |
| **How long** | duration | How long does the journey take? |
| **How often** | frequency | How often does the bus come? |
| **How far** | distance | How far is the hospital? |
| **What time** | exact time | What time does the train leave? |

## The formula

<div class="ssp-formula">Wh- word + <em>helper (is/are/do/does/did/can/will)</em> + subject + verb … ?</div>

| Wh- | Helper | Subject | Verb / rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | **is** | the ticket counter | ? |
| When | **does** | the train | leave? |
| How much | **did** | you | pay? |
| Why | **are** | you | late? |
| Which platform | **does** | the Chennai train | leave from? |
| How long | **will** | it | take? |
| What | **can** | I | do? |

## From statement to question: step by step

Statement: **The bus leaves at 6.**

1. Find what you want to ask about: *at 6* → a time → **When / What time**
2. Add the helper: the verb is "leaves" (present, he/she/it) → **does**
3. Base verb: leaves → **leave**
4. Question: **What time does the bus leave?**

| Statement | Ask about | Question |
|---|---|---|
| She lives **in Pune**. | place | **Where does she live?** |
| The room costs **₹3,000**. | price | **How much does the room cost?** |
| I'm late **because of traffic**. | reason | **Why are you late?** |
| **Ravi** fixed the bug. | person (subject) | **Who fixed the bug?** |
| They met **last week**. | time | **When did they meet?** |

!!! info "Special case: who / what as the subject"
    When **who** or **what** is the *subject*, don't add do/does/did:
    ✅ **Who called you?** (someone called) · ✅ **What happened?** · ❌ *Who did call you?*

## Indirect questions: more polite

Start with a polite phrase, then use **statement order** (no do/does/did):

| Direct | Polite (indirect) |
|---|---|
| Where is the station? | Could you tell me **where the station is**? |
| What time does it open? | Do you know **what time it opens**? |
| How much does it cost? | Can I ask **how much it costs**? |
| When will it be ready? | Could you let me know **when it will be ready**? |

Use indirect questions with strangers, officials and senior people.

## Conversation: at a railway enquiry counter

```dialogue
You: Excuse me. Which train goes to Mysuru this evening?
Clerk: There's the Chamundi Express at 6:15.
You: Which platform does it leave from?
Clerk: Platform 8.
You: How long does the journey take?
Clerk: About three hours.
You: How much is a second-class ticket?
Clerk: ₹110.
You: And how often do trains go to Mysuru on Sundays?
Clerk: Every two hours, roughly.
You: Could you tell me where the ticket counter for reserved tickets is?
Clerk: It's on the first floor, near the escalator.
You: Thank you very much!
```

## Practice

**1. Choose the question word:** what, where, when, why, who, which, how much, how long.

1. ______ is your office? (place) 2. ______ is this shirt? (price) 3. ______ did you call me? (reason)
4. ______ colour do you want: red or blue? 5. ______ does the movie last?

??? success "Answers"
    1. Where 2. How much 3. Why 4. Which 5. How long

**2. Make a question about the bold part.**

1. The meeting starts **at 4**. 2. He bought **a new phone**. 3. We stayed **for three days**. 4. **Meena** booked the hotel.

??? success "Answers"
    1. What time does the meeting start? 2. What did he buy? 3. How long did you stay? 4. Who booked the hotel?

**3. Make these polite.** "Where is the washroom?" / "When will my order arrive?"

??? success "Answers"
    Could you tell me where the washroom is? / Could you let me know when my order will arrive?
