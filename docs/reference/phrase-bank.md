# Phrase Bank

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Learn 5 phrases a week and use each one at least twice in real conversations.** Within 3 months you'll
have 60 phrases you can say without thinking.
</div>

=== "Agreeing"

    - "That makes sense."
    - "I'm fully on board with that."
    - "Good point. Let's go with it."
    - "I agree, and I'd add one thing…"

=== "Disagreeing politely"

    - "I see it a bit differently."
    - "I'm not sure that'll work, because…"
    - "Can I offer another perspective?"
    - "I understand the reasoning. My concern is…"
    - "What if we tried…?"

=== "Asking for clarity"

    - "Could you walk me through that again?"
    - "What do you mean by 'scalable' here?"
    - "Just to make sure I understand: you're saying…?"
    - "Could you give me an example?"

=== "Buying time"

    - "Good question. Let me think about that."
    - "I'd like to check the data before I answer."
    - "Can I get back to you by end of day?"

=== "Ownership"

    - "I'll take care of it."
    - "That's on me. Here's how I'll fix it."
    - "I'll own the follow-up."
    - "You can count on me for this."

=== "Apologising (when you should)"

    - "I apologise. I missed that."
    - "You're right, I should have flagged it earlier."
    - "Sorry for the confusion. Here's the correct version."

    **Instead of over-apologising:** "Thanks for your patience" · "Thanks for catching that" ·
    "Appreciate you waiting."

=== "Asking for help"

    - "Could I get your input on something?"
    - "Do you have 10 minutes to look at this with me?"
    - "I've tried X and Y. What would you suggest next?"

=== "Saying no"

    - "I'd love to help, but I'm at capacity this week."
    - "Not right now. Could we revisit next sprint?"
    - "I can do X, but not Y."
    - "Which should I deprioritise to make room for this?"

=== "Giving praise"

    - "Great work on the release. The rollback plan was really thorough."
    - "Thank you, you saved us hours."
    - "I really liked how you explained that to the client."

=== "Closing"

    - "Let's summarise what we've agreed."
    - "Who's doing what by when?"
    - "I'll send notes shortly."
    - "Thanks, everyone, this was really helpful."

## Upgrade your vocabulary

| Basic | Professional |
|---|---|
| "I think" | "In my view" / "My recommendation is" |
| "Very important" | "Critical" / "High-priority" |
| "Problem" | "Issue" / "Challenge" / "Blocker" |
| "Fix" | "Resolve" / "Address" |
| "Big" | "Significant" / "Substantial" |
| "Check" | "Verify" / "Review" |
| "Tell" | "Inform" / "Update" / "Flag" |
| "Start" | "Kick off" / "Initiate" |
| "Get" | "Obtain" / "Receive" |
| "Do the needful" | "Please take care of this" / "Could you please [specific action]" |
| "Revert back" | "Reply" / "Get back to me" |
| "Prepone" | "Move earlier" / "Bring forward" |
| "Kindly do it" | "Could you please…" |
| "Same to you" (for "Have a good day") | "Thanks, you too!" |

!!! tip "Common Indian-English phrases to swap in global teams"
    "Do the needful", "revert back", "prepone", "out of station", "pass out (graduate)" are
    understood in India but can confuse global colleagues. Use the professional column above.
