# Daily Practice Checklist

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Ten minutes a day beats a two-hour workshop once a year.** Print this page or pin it.
</div>

## Every day (10 minutes)

- [ ] Speak at least **once in every meeting**
- [ ] Put the **main point first** in every message
- [ ] **Rewrite one message** to be shorter before sending
- [ ] Learn & use **one phrase** from the [Phrase Bank](phrase-bank.md)
- [ ] One **5-minute AI role-play** with the [Master Prompt](../master-prompt.md)
- [ ] **Journal 3 lines:** what I said · how it landed · what I'll try next

## Every week

- [ ] Prepare a written **1:1 agenda** for your manager
- [ ] Have **one conversation outside your team**
- [ ] **Record yourself** for 2 minutes explaining a technical concept
- [ ] Give **specific praise** to at least two people
- [ ] Try **one real-life scenario**: order at a café, ask directions, small talk on a train

## Every month

- [ ] Redo the **[self-assessment](../foundations/improving-soft-skills.md#step-1-self-assessment)**
- [ ] Ask for **feedback on your communication** from one person
- [ ] Do **one visibility action**: demo, presentation, write-up, knowledge-share
- [ ] Have the **one difficult conversation** you've been postponing
- [ ] Update your **wins file**

## Before any important conversation

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Goal <span>— what do I want them to know / decide / do?</span></div>
<div>2. Audience <span>— what do they care about? (altitude rule)</span></div>
<div>3. Headline <span>— my point in one sentence</span></div>
<div>4. Evidence <span>— 2–3 facts or numbers</span></div>
<div>5. Ask <span>— the exact next step</span></div>
<div>6. Breathe <span>— 4-4-6 breathing, then go</span></div>
</div>
