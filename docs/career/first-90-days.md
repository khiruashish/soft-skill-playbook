# First 90 Days at a New Job

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**First impressions are formed in weeks and last for years.** Spend the first 90 days listening,
building relationships, and delivering one visible early win.
</div>

## The 30-60-90 plan

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>Days 1–30: Learn <span>— people, product, codebase, how decisions get made</span></div>
<div>Days 31–60: Contribute <span>— ship real work, join discussions, own something small</span></div>
<div>Days 61–90: Lead <span>— propose improvements, own a feature, mentor where you can</span></div>
</div>

## Introducing yourself

=== "To the team (stand-up / first meeting)"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Hi everyone, I'm Ashish. I've joined as a senior backend engineer. **Before this, I spent 6
    years building payment systems at XYZ.** Outside work, I love cricket and cooking. **I'm looking
    forward to learning how things work here**, so please bear with my questions!"
    </div>

=== "Slack / Teams intro"

    ```text
    👋 Hi all! I'm Ashish, joining the Checkout team as a Senior Backend Engineer.
    Previously: 6 yrs on payments at XYZ (Java, Kafka, Postgres).
    Fun fact: I've visited 14 states on train journeys 🚆
    Would love a 15-min coffee chat with anyone. Happy to learn how your team works!
    ```

=== "To your new manager (first 1:1)"

    - "**What does success look like** for me at 30, 60 and 90 days?"
    - "**How do you prefer to communicate**: Slack, email, 1:1s? How often do you want updates?"
    - "**Who are the key people** I should meet in my first month?"
    - "What's the **biggest challenge** the team faces right now?"
    - "Is there a **small, valuable task** I could pick up as a first win?"

## Questions to ask in your first month

| Area | Questions |
|---|---|
| Product | "Who are our users? What do they complain about most?" |
| Codebase | "Which parts are fragile? Where is the documentation?" |
| Process | "How does code go from PR to production? What's the on-call setup?" |
| People | "Who's the go-to person for payments / infra / data?" |
| Culture | "How are decisions made? How do people disagree here?" |
| History | "Why was it built this way?" (ask out of curiosity, not to judge) |

## Senior hires: avoid the classic trap

!!! warning "Don't start with \"At my last company we did it better…\""
    Even when you're right, it sounds like criticism. Try:

    - "**Help me understand** why we chose this approach."
    - "**I've seen a pattern** that worked well for this problem. Would it be useful if I wrote it up?"
    - Earn trust with **small wins first**; propose big changes after 60 days.

## Habits for the first 90 days

- [ ] **Take notes** in every meeting; start a personal "how things work here" doc
- [ ] **Coffee chats:** 2 per week with people across teams
- [ ] **Fix the onboarding docs** wherever you hit a gap (an easy first PR)
- [ ] Ship your **first PR in week 1**, even if it's tiny
- [ ] **Send a weekly update** to your manager for the first 2 months
- [ ] **Ask for feedback at day 30**: "How am I doing? Anything I should adjust?"
- [ ] **Remember names:** say them back when introduced, and write them down after

## Day-90 conversation with your manager

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"It's been 90 days, and I'd like to reflect together. **Here's what I've delivered**: the coupon
validator and the on-call runbook. **Here's what I've learned** about the team. **What's your
feedback, and what should my priorities be for the next 6 months?**"
</div>
