# Cover Letter

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** write a short cover letter or application email that explains why you, why this role
and why this company, in under 250 words. Many recruiters skim, so be specific and brief.
</div>

## When do you need one?

| Situation | Cover letter? |
|---|---|
| The application asks for it | **Yes**, always |
| Applying by email | **Yes**, the email body *is* your cover letter (shorter) |
| Career switch or gap | **Yes**, to explain it positively |
| Applying with a referral | Optional; a short note to the hiring manager helps |
| Quick portal "easy apply" | Usually optional |

## The 4-paragraph structure

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Hook <span>— the role you're applying for, and one line on why you're a strong fit</span></div>
<div>2. Proof <span>— 2–3 achievements that match the job description, with numbers</span></div>
<div>3. Why them <span>— something specific about the company, product or team</span></div>
<div>4. Close <span>— thank them, and a clear, confident next step</span></div>
</div>

## Example cover letter

```text
Dear Ms. Rao,

I'm applying for the Senior Backend Engineer role on your payments platform team (job ID 4471).
With nine years of building high-scale payment systems in Java and Kafka, I'm confident I can
help your team scale reliably.

At XYZ Technologies, I led the re-architecture of our checkout using the outbox pattern, cutting
failed orders by 60%. I also reduced payment failures from 3% to 0.5% by adding retries and a
backup gateway, and I mentored four engineers, two of whom have since been promoted.

I've followed Brightsoft's move to real-time settlements, and your engineering blog post on
idempotent payment APIs matched exactly how I like to design systems. I'd love to contribute to
that work.

Thank you for considering my application. I'd welcome the chance to discuss how I can help your
team, and I'm available for a conversation any day next week.

Sincerely,
Ashish Gupta
+91 98450 12345 · ashish.gupta@example.com · linkedin.com/in/ashish-example
```

## Application email (short version)

```text
Subject: Application — Senior Backend Engineer (ID 4471) — Ashish Gupta

Dear Ms. Rao,

I'd like to apply for the Senior Backend Engineer role. I have nine years of experience
building payment systems in Java and Kafka, most recently leading a checkout re-architecture
that cut failed orders by 60%.

I've attached my resume. I'd be glad to discuss how I can help your payments team.

Best regards,
Ashish Gupta
+91 98450 12345
```

## Phrases that help

| Part | Useful phrases |
|---|---|
| Opening | I'm writing to apply for… · I was excited to see your opening for… · [Name] suggested I contact you about… |
| Fit | With X years of experience in… · My background in… makes me a strong fit for… |
| Proof | At [company], I led… which resulted in… · I was responsible for… and achieved… |
| Why them | I've followed your work on… · Your focus on… matches my interest in… |
| Gap or switch | After a planned career break to care for my family, I… · I'm moving from QA into development, and over the last two years I've… |
| Close | I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss… · Thank you for your time and consideration. |

## Mistakes to avoid

- ❌ "To whom it may concern" (find the hiring manager's name if you can; otherwise "Dear Hiring Manager")
- ❌ Repeating your whole resume
- ❌ Generic praise ("Your company is a leading company")
- ❌ Talking only about what **you** want, not what **they** need
- ❌ Over 1 page, or the wrong company name (check before sending!)

## Conversation: asking about the hiring manager's name

```dialogue
You: Hi Riya, I'm preparing my application for the Senior Backend Engineer role. Could I ask who the hiring manager is?
Recruiter: Sure, it's Kavita Rao, the engineering manager for payments.
You: Thank you. Should I send a cover letter, or is the resume enough?
Recruiter: A short cover letter helps for this team. Keep it to one page.
You: Got it. Is there anything specific the team is looking for?
Recruiter: Experience with high-volume payment systems, and strong communication.
You: That's very helpful. I'll send everything by tomorrow morning.
Recruiter: Perfect, thanks Ashish.
```

## Practice

Write the **Proof** paragraph for a role you want: 2 achievements with numbers that match the job description.
