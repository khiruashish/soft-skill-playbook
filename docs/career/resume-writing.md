# Resume Writing

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** write a clear, one-to-two-page resume (CV) that shows results, passes applicant tracking
systems (ATS), and makes a recruiter want to call you.
</div>

## How resumes are read

A recruiter often spends **under a minute** on the first look, and many companies first filter resumes
with an **ATS** (software that matches keywords). So your resume must be **easy to scan** and use the
**same words as the job description**.

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Header <span>— name, phone, email, city, LinkedIn, GitHub/portfolio</span></div>
<div>2. Summary <span>— 2–3 lines: who you are, your strengths, what you want</span></div>
<div>3. Skills <span>— grouped: languages, frameworks, cloud, tools</span></div>
<div>4. Experience <span>— newest first; 3–5 result bullets per role</span></div>
<div>5. Projects, education, certifications <span>— the most relevant only</span></div>
</div>

## Length & format

| Experience | Length |
|---|---|
| Fresher / 0–3 years | 1 page |
| 3–10 years | 1–2 pages |
| 10+ years | 2 pages (focus on the last 8–10 years) |

- **Simple layout**, one column, standard fonts, clear headings; avoid tables, images and text boxes (ATS can't read them)
- Save as **PDF** (unless the job asks for Word); file name: `Ashish-Gupta-Backend-Engineer.pdf`
- **No** photo, date of birth, religion, marital status or full address unless the country/company requires them

## The bullet formula

<div class="ssp-formula">Action verb + <em>what you did</em> + how + measurable result</div>

| ❌ Weak bullet | ✅ Strong bullet |
|---|---|
| Responsible for backend development. | **Built** 12 Spring Boot microservices for order and payment flows, **handling 2M+ requests a day**. |
| Worked on performance improvement. | **Reduced** API p95 latency **from 1.4 s to 300 ms** by adding Redis caching and fixing N+1 queries. |
| Did code reviews and mentoring. | **Mentored** 4 junior engineers; **2 were promoted** within a year. |
| Handled deployments. | **Automated** CI/CD with GitHub Actions, **cutting release time from 2 hours to 18 minutes**. |

**No exact number?** Use scale, frequency or comparison: "for 40+ stores", "weekly", "3× faster",
"first in the team to…".

**Action verbs:** built · designed · led · launched · reduced · improved · automated · migrated ·
scaled · optimised · delivered · mentored · introduced · resolved

## Summary: examples

=== "Experienced"

    ```text
    Senior Backend Engineer with 9 years building high-scale payment and e-commerce systems in
    Java, Kafka and AWS. Led a checkout re-architecture that cut failed orders by 60%. Looking
    for a Staff Engineer role focused on distributed systems.
    ```

=== "Fresher"

    ```text
    Computer Science graduate (2026) with strong Java and Spring Boot skills. Built a grocery
    delivery app with 1,000+ test users as a final-year project and completed a 6-month backend
    internship at XYZ. Looking for a Software Engineer role.
    ```

=== "Career switch"

    ```text
    QA engineer moving into backend development, with 4 years of test automation in Java and
    2 years building internal Spring Boot tools used by 60 engineers. AWS Certified Developer.
    ```

## Tailor it for each job

1. Copy the **job description**; highlight skills and keywords.
2. Make sure the **same words** appear in your summary, skills and bullets (only if true).
3. Move the **most relevant** experience and projects to the top.
4. Remove things that don't help **this** application.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ Spelling and grammar errors (ask someone to proofread)
- ❌ "I", "me", "my" in bullets (start with the verb instead)
- ❌ Long paragraphs instead of bullets
- ❌ Listing every technology you've touched once
- ❌ Unprofessional email address
- ❌ Exaggerating or claiming skills you can't discuss in an interview

## Conversation: getting resume feedback from a senior

```dialogue
You: Hi Meena, could you take a quick look at my resume? I'm applying for senior roles.
Meena: Sure, send it over.
(Ten minutes later)
Meena: It's a good start. But most of your bullets describe duties, not results.
You: Could you give me an example?
Meena: "Worked on the payment service." What changed because of your work?
You: We reduced payment failures from 3% to 0.5% after I added retries and a backup gateway.
Meena: That's the bullet! Put the number in it.
You: Got it. Anything else?
Meena: Your summary is five lines. Cut it to two or three, and match the keywords in the job description.
You: That's really helpful. I'll update it tonight and send you the new version.
```
