# Salary Negotiation

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Companies expect you to negotiate, and a calm, data-backed ask is professional, not rude.** A few
uncomfortable minutes can change your earnings for years, because every future raise builds on this
number.
</div>

## The negotiation flow

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Research <span>— know your market value (range, not a single number)</span></div>
<div>2. Prepare <span>— your wins, your target, your walk-away number</span></div>
<div>3. Delay the number <span>— let them anchor first when possible</span></div>
<div>4. Respond <span>— thank, show enthusiasm, then counter with data</span></div>
<div>5. Trade <span>— negotiate the whole package, not only base pay</span></div>
<div>6. Close <span>— confirm everything in writing</span></div>
</div>

## Step 1: Know your numbers

| Number | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Market range** | What similar roles pay (level, city, company type) | ₹45–60 LPA |
| **Target** | What you'll ask for: upper part of the range, backed by data | ₹58 LPA |
| **Walk-away** | Below this, you decline. Decide before the conversation. | ₹50 LPA |

**Sources:** salary sites (Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, Levels.fyi), recruiters, trusted peers,
industry reports. Compare the **same level and city**, and compare **total compensation**, not just base.

## Step 2: Build your value case

Write down 3–5 achievements **with numbers**:

- "Led the checkout re-architecture, which cut failures 60% and protected ~₹2 Cr in sale-day revenue."
- "Reduced cloud costs by ₹35 lakh/year."
- "Mentored 4 engineers; 2 were promoted."
- "Cut deploy time from 2 hours to 18 minutes."

## Scripts: new job offer

=== "\"What are your salary expectations?\" (early)"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'd like to understand the role and responsibilities better first. **Could you share the budgeted
    range for this position?**"
    </div>

    If they insist:

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Based on my research for senior backend roles in Bangalore with my experience, **I'm looking in
    the range of ₹55 to 62 lakh total compensation**, depending on the overall package."
    </div>

    Give a range whose **bottom** is still a number you'd be happy with.

=== "\"What's your current salary?\""

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'd prefer to focus on the value of this role. **My expectation is based on the market and what I
    bring**, which is around ₹58 lakh."
    </div>

    (In some places, employers may ask for payslips. Be honest if you share. Never inflate.)

=== "Responding to the offer"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Thank you, **I'm really excited about this role and the team.** I've reviewed the offer. Given my
    experience leading high-scale payment systems and the market range for this level, **I was
    expecting closer to ₹58 lakh. Is there flexibility on the base?**"
    </div>

    Then **stop talking** and let them respond. The silence works in your favour.

=== "They say \"This is our best offer\""

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I understand the base may be fixed. **Could we look at other parts of the package**: a joining
    bonus, extra ESOPs, or a salary review after 6 months instead of 12?"
    </div>

=== "Competing offer"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I want to be transparent: **I have another offer at ₹60 lakh.** This role is my first choice
    because of the product and the team. **If you can get closer to that, I'm ready to accept this
    week.**"
    </div>

    Only say this if it's **true**. Bluffing can backfire badly.

=== "Accepting"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Thank you, that works for me. **I'm happy to accept.** Could you send the updated offer letter
    with the base, joining bonus and ESOP details so I can sign it?"
    </div>

## Scripts: raise at your current company

=== "Asking for the meeting"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Could we set up 30 minutes next week to discuss **my compensation and growth**? I'd like to
    prepare properly, so I wanted to give you notice."
    </div>

=== "Making the case"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "Over the last year I've **led the checkout migration, reduced cloud costs by ₹35 lakh, and
    mentored four engineers.** My responsibilities have grown to include architecture for two teams.
    Based on that and market data for this level, **I'd like to discuss an adjustment to around
    ₹58 lakh.** What would it take to make that happen?"
    </div>

=== "If the answer is \"not now\""

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I understand the budget timing. **Could we agree on specific goals and a date to revisit**, say
    in 3 months? I'd also appreciate it in writing so we're both clear."
    </div>

## Negotiate the whole package

| Component | What to ask |
|---|---|
| Base salary | The main number; every future hike builds on it |
| Joining / sign-on bonus | Covers bonus or ESOPs you lose by leaving |
| Variable / performance bonus | Target %, how it's measured, how often it's paid out in practice |
| ESOPs / RSUs | Number, vesting schedule, cliff, current valuation |
| Title / level | Affects future pay and your next job |
| Notice-period buyout | If your current notice is long |
| Remote / hybrid | Days in office, home-office allowance |
| Learning budget | Certifications, conferences |
| Review timeline | Early review at 6 months |
| Relocation | Travel, temporary housing |

## Phrases that help and phrases that hurt

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use instead |
|---|---|
| "I need more money because my rent went up." | "Based on my impact and market data…" |
| "Whatever you think is fair." | "I'm looking for around ₹58 lakh." |
| "I'll leave if I don't get it." (threat) | "I want to grow here. What would make this possible?" |
| "Sorry to ask, but…" | "I'd like to discuss…" |
| "Okay." (accepting immediately) | "Thank you. Can I take a day to review the full package?" |
| A single exact low number | A researched range or a confident specific number |

## Golden rules

!!! success "Do"
    - Be **enthusiastic about the role** and firm on the number in the same conversation.
    - **Ask for time:** "Can I get back to you by Thursday?" is normal.
    - **Get everything in writing** before you resign from your current job.
    - Negotiate **once, well**. Avoid going back again and again.

!!! danger "Don't"
    - Lie about offers or current salary.
    - Negotiate over chat or messages for the main discussion; prefer a call.
    - Accept a counter-offer from your current employer purely for money. The reasons you wanted to
      leave usually remain.
    - Burn bridges if it doesn't work out: "Thank you for the offer. I'll have to decline this time,
      but I'd love to stay in touch."

## Negotiation role-play prompt

```text
Role-play a salary negotiation. You are an HR manager at a product company. Offer me ₹48 LPA for a
Senior Backend Engineer role (my target is ₹58 LPA, walk-away ₹50 LPA). Push back realistically:
"budget is fixed", "that's above our band", "we need an answer today". After I type DEBRIEF, score
my negotiation and rewrite my weakest reply.
```

## Practice conversation: Countering an offer

```dialogue
HR: We're happy to offer you ₹48 lakh per year.
You: Thank you, I'm really excited about this role and the team.
HR: Great! Can we expect your acceptance this week?
You: I'd like to discuss the base salary first. Based on my experience and the market for this level, I was expecting closer to ₹58 lakh.
HR: That's above our band for this role.
You: I understand. Is there any flexibility, perhaps with a joining bonus or extra ESOPs?
HR: I could check on a joining bonus of ₹4 lakh and a review after six months.
You: That would help. Could you also check if the base can go to ₹52 lakh? If so, I'm ready to accept this week.
HR: Let me check and get back to you tomorrow.
```
