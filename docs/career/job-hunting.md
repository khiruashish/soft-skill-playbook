# Job Hunting

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** plan your job search, talk to recruiters with confidence, ask for referrals, follow up
without being pushy, and handle offers, rejections and notice periods professionally.
</div>

## The job-search pipeline

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Target <span>— 2–3 role types, preferred companies, salary range, location</span></div>
<div>2. Prepare <span>— resume, cover letter, LinkedIn, 6–8 STAR stories, a portfolio or GitHub</span></div>
<div>3. Reach out <span>— referrals first, then recruiters, then job portals</span></div>
<div>4. Interview <span>— screening, technical, managerial, HR</span></div>
<div>5. Decide <span>— compare offers, negotiate, accept, resign well</span></div>
</div>

!!! tip "Referrals work best"
    Applications with a referral are usually far more likely to get an interview than cold applications
    on a portal. Spend most of your time on **people**, not portals.

## Key words

| Word / phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| job description (JD) / requisition / job ID | the role details / its number |
| shortlisted / screening call | selected for the next step / first call |
| CTC / fixed / variable / in-hand | total cost to company / guaranteed / bonus / monthly take-home |
| notice period / buyout / last working day (LWD) | time before you can join / paying to leave early / final day |
| offer letter / background verification (BGV) | the written offer / checks of your history |
| counter-offer | your current company offers more to keep you |
| on hold / position closed | paused / no longer hiring |

## The recruiter screening call

| Recruiter asks | Good answer |
|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself. | (60 seconds: present → past → future; see [Interviews](interviews.md)) |
| Why are you looking for a change? | I'm looking for larger-scale systems and a path to a Staff role. |
| What's your current CTC? | (In some places this question isn't allowed. You can say:) I'd prefer to focus on the role's range. My expectation is around ₹55–60 lakh. |
| What's your expected CTC? | Based on the market for this level, ₹55–60 lakh, depending on the overall package. |
| What's your notice period? | 60 days, and it's negotiable to 45. I can also discuss a buyout. |
| Do you have other offers? | I'm in the final rounds with two companies. (Only say this if it's true.) |
| Are you open to relocation / office days? | Yes, Bengaluru works, and three office days a week is fine. |

## Questions to ask recruiters

- **What's the team and product** I'd be working on?
- **What does the interview process** look like, and how long does it take?
- **What's the budgeted range** for this role?
- **Why is the position open**: new role or replacement?
- **What's the work model**: remote, hybrid, office?
- **When can I expect** to hear back after each round?

## Following up (without being pushy)

=== "After applying (1 week)"

    ```text
    Hi Riya, I applied for the Senior Backend Engineer role (ID 4471) last Monday. I'm very
    interested in the payments platform team. Is there any update on the shortlisting?
    Happy to share anything else you need. Thanks!
    ```

=== "After an interview (24 hours)"

    ```text
    Hi Karan, thank you for the conversation yesterday. I enjoyed discussing the event-driven
    redesign of the order service, and it made me even more excited about the role. Please let
    me know if you need anything else from me. Looking forward to the next steps.
    ```

=== "No reply after the promised date"

    ```text
    Hi Riya, just checking in on the final-round feedback. You'd mentioned we might hear by
    Friday. I have another process moving forward, so an update would really help me plan.
    Thank you!
    ```

## Handling outcomes

| Situation | What to say |
|---|---|
| Rejection | "Thank you for letting me know. Could you share any feedback that would help me improve? I'd be glad to be considered for future roles." |
| Offer you want | "Thank you, I'm really excited! Could you send the detailed offer so I can review the full package?" (Then see [Salary Negotiation](salary-negotiation.md).) |
| Offer you'll decline | "Thank you for the offer and your time. After careful thought, I've decided to accept another role that's a closer fit. I hope our paths cross again." |
| Need more time | "Could I have until Monday to decide? I want to make a careful decision." |
| Joining date pushback | "My notice is 60 days. I'll request an early release and can share an update in two weeks." |

## Conversation 1: recruiter screening call

```dialogue
Recruiter: Hi Ashish, this is Riya from TalentBridge. Is this a good time to talk?
You: Hi Riya, yes, I have about 15 minutes.
Recruiter: Great. I'm hiring a Senior Backend Engineer for a fintech client. Are you open to a change?
You: Yes, for the right role. Could you tell me a bit about the product and the team?
Recruiter: It's a payments platform team of eight people, mostly Java and Kafka.
You: That's very relevant to my experience. What's the interview process like?
Recruiter: A coding round, a system design round, a managerial round, and HR. Usually two to three weeks.
You: And what's the budgeted range?
Recruiter: Up to ₹58 lakh, depending on the interviews. What's your notice period?
You: Sixty days, but it's negotiable to 45.
Recruiter: Perfect. I'll share the JD and schedule the first round.
You: Thank you. Please send the JD to my email, and I'll confirm a slot today.
```

## Conversation 2: asking a former colleague for a referral

```dialogue
You: Hi Arjun! It's been a while. How's life at Brightsoft?
Arjun: Hey Ashish! Really good, thanks. What's new with you?
You: I'm exploring new roles. I saw that your team is hiring a senior backend engineer.
Arjun: Oh yes, we need people badly. Are you interested?
You: Very. Would you be comfortable referring me? I can send you my CV and a short summary.
Arjun: Of course. Send them over and I'll submit it today.
You: Thank you so much. Is there anything about the team I should know before the interviews?
Arjun: They care a lot about system design and clear communication. You'll be fine!
You: That's really helpful. I owe you a coffee!
```

## Weekly job-search routine

- [ ] **5** personalised referral or networking messages
- [ ] **5–10** targeted applications (not 50 random ones)
- [ ] **2** mock interviews (with a friend or the [master prompt](../master-prompt.md))
- [ ] **1** new STAR story written down
- [ ] Update a simple tracker: company · role · contact · stage · next step · date
