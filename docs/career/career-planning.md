## Common paths for software professionals

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-code-braces:{ .lg .middle } __Individual contributor__

    ---

    Senior → Staff → Principal. Deep technical ownership, architecture, influence across teams.

-   :material-account-group:{ .lg .middle } __Management__

    ---

    Team lead → Engineering manager → Director. Growing people, hiring, delivery, strategy.

-   :material-swap-horizontal:{ .lg .middle } __Specialist / switch__

    ---

    Cloud, security, data, ML, product management, developer relations, solutions architecture.

-   :material-rocket-launch:{ .lg .middle } __Independent__

    ---

    Freelancing, consulting, starting a company, teaching.

</div>

## Build a learning plan

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Goal <span>— "Move to a Staff Engineer role in 18 months"</span></div>
<div>2. Gaps <span>— 2–3 skills or experiences missing (e.g. system design, leading cross-team work)</span></div>
<div>3. Actions <span>— a course, a certification, a project at work, a mentor</span></div>
<div>4. Proof <span>— something visible: a launched project, a talk, a doc, a certificate</span></div>
<div>5. Review <span>— check progress every quarter, with your manager or mentor</span></div>
</div>

!!! tip "Learn at work first"
    The fastest growth comes from **stretch projects at work**, not only from courses. Ask for work
    that uses the skill you want to build.

## Conversations to have

=== "With your manager"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'd like to talk about my longer-term growth. **In two years, I'd like to be working as a
    solutions architect.** What experience would I need, and **are there projects here** where I
    could start building it?"
    </div>

=== "Asking for a learning budget"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'd like to take the AWS Solutions Architect certification. **It's ₹25,000** including the exam.
    It would help with our cloud migration next year. **Could the team's learning budget cover it?**"
    </div>

=== "Exploring a switch"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'm curious about product management. **Could I shadow** our PM in a few customer calls and
    planning meetings next month? I think it would also help me build better features."
    </div>

## Conversation: career chat with a mentor

```dialogue
Mentor: So, where do you see yourself in three years?
You: Honestly, I'm not sure whether I want to manage people or stay technical.
Mentor: That's a common question. What do you enjoy most in your current work?
You: Designing systems and helping juniors when they're stuck. I don't enjoy status meetings.
Mentor: That sounds more like a Staff Engineer path than management. Have you led a cross-team project yet?
You: Not really. Mostly work within my own team.
Mentor: That's the gap. Ask your manager for a project that involves two or three teams.
You: That makes sense. I'll also start writing design docs for the wider org.
Mentor: Great. Let's check in again in three months and see how it's going.
```
