# Asking for Leave & Setting Boundaries

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Rested engineers write better code.** Asking for leave and protecting your time are part of being a
professional. Plan ahead, cover your work, and say it clearly without guilt.
</div>

## Asking for planned leave

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Check <span>— team calendar, release dates, on-call rota</span></div>
<div>2. Ask early <span>— 2–4 weeks ahead for a week off; more for long leave</span></div>
<div>3. Plan cover <span>— who handles what while you're away</span></div>
<div>4. Confirm in writing <span>— leave system + calendar + team channel</span></div>
<div>5. Hand over <span>— notes before you go; out-of-office on</span></div>
</div>

=== "Asking your manager"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'd like to take leave from **15th to 22nd December** for a family trip. I've checked: it's
    after the release, and Ravi can cover my on-call shift. **I'll finish the search work and hand
    over notes before I go.** Does that work for you?"
    </div>

    Note the wording: **"I'd like to take"**, not "Can I please, if it's okay, maybe…"

=== "Sick leave (same day)"

    ```text
    Hi Priya, I'm unwell today (fever) and won't be able to work. I'll take sick leave.
    Urgent items: PR #231 needs a review — Meena, could you take it?
    I'll update you tomorrow morning.
    ```

    Short and clear. You don't need to give medical details.

=== "Emergency leave"

    ```text
    Hi Priya, I have a family emergency and need to leave right away. I'll be offline for
    the rest of today, possibly tomorrow. Ravi has context on the cart bug.
    I'll update you as soon as I can.
    ```

=== "Long leave (wedding, parental, medical)"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I wanted to give you early notice: **I'm planning parental leave from March for 3 months.**
    I'd like to plan the handover together. Could we put together a coverage plan over the next
    few weeks?"
    </div>

## Out-of-office message

```text
Thanks for your message. I'm on leave from 15–22 Dec with limited access to email.
For urgent checkout issues, please contact Ravi Kumar (ravi@example.com).
For everything else, I'll reply after I return on 23 Dec.
```

## Handover note before leave

```text
Leave handover — Ashish (15–22 Dec)
• Search filters: merged; feature flag OFF — Meena to enable after QA sign-off
• Bug #341: root cause found, fix in PR #245 — needs one more review
• On-call: swapped with Ravi (15–18 Dec)
• Don't panic if: nightly report job warns about latency — known issue, ticket #350
```

## Setting boundaries

### Saying no to after-hours work (politely)

=== "Late-evening request"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'm logging off for the day. **I'll pick this up first thing at 9:30 tomorrow.** If it's a
    production issue, the on-call engineer is Meena."
    </div>

=== "Weekend messages"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    (Monday) "Saw your weekend message. I'll have it done by noon today. **Going forward, if
    something is urgent on a weekend, a call is the best way to reach me**, so I don't miss it, and
    I can switch off when it's not."
    </div>

=== "Too many meetings"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'm in meetings 5–6 hours a day, and coding work is slipping. **Could I block 10 am–1 pm as
    focus time**, and drop the meetings where I'm optional?"
    </div>

=== "Being pulled into everything"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I'm happy to help, but if I take this, the search release slips. **Can we check with Priya
    which one is the priority?**"
    </div>

### Boundary habits

- Set **working hours** in Slack/Teams and calendar
- Turn off **notifications** after hours (except on-call)
- **Schedule-send** late-night messages for the morning so others don't feel pressure
- Block **focus time** on your calendar, and protect it
- Say **"Let me check my calendar"** before agreeing to anything big

!!! tip "Boundaries for leads"
    Your behaviour sets the norm. If you message at midnight, your team thinks they must answer at
    midnight. Use schedule-send, and **take your own leave visibly**.

## Practice conversation: Asking for leave

```dialogue
You: I'd like to take leave from the 15th to the 22nd of December for a family trip.
Manager: That's right after the release, isn't it?
You: Yes, the release is on the 12th. Ravi has agreed to cover my on-call shift.
Manager: What about the search work?
You: I'll finish it before I go and write a handover note.
Manager: Sounds good. Approved. Enjoy your trip!
You: Thank you!
```
