# Time Management

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Today's goal:** prioritise your work, protect focus time, and, most importantly for communication,
**talk** about time: giving realistic estimates, saying no, renegotiating deadlines and updating people
before they have to ask.
</div>

## Prioritise: the urgent–important grid

| | **Urgent** | **Not urgent** |
|---|---|---|
| **Important** | **Do now:** production incident, release blocker | **Schedule:** design doc, learning, tech debt, 1:1s |
| **Not important** | **Delegate or quick reply:** most pings, some meetings | **Drop:** endless scrolling, optional meetings |

Most real growth happens in **important, not urgent**. Block time for it, or it never happens.

## Daily and weekly habits

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>Plan (5 min each morning) <span>— pick your top 3 for the day</span></div>
<div>Focus blocks <span>— 90–120 minutes, notifications off, calendar blocked</span></div>
<div>Batch small things <span>— emails and Slack 3–4 times a day, not all day</span></div>
<div>End-of-day review <span>— what's done, what moves to tomorrow, who needs an update</span></div>
<div>Weekly plan (Friday or Monday) <span>— priorities, deadlines, meetings to decline</span></div>

## Talking about time

=== "Estimating"

    - "**It'll take three to five days**, assuming the API contract doesn't change."
    - "**I need half a day** to investigate before I can give you a reliable estimate."
    - "**The fastest I can do** with good quality is Thursday."

=== "Prioritising with your manager"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "I have three things this week: the search release, the bug from the client, and the hiring
    interviews. **I can't do all three well.** **Which one should come first?**"
    </div>

=== "Protecting focus time"

    - "I'm **heads-down until 1 pm** on the release. Can I get back to you after that?"
    - "I've blocked **10 to 12 for focus time**. Could we meet at 3 instead?"
    - "**Do you need me** in this meeting, or can I read the notes?"

=== "Running late"

    <div class="ssp-say" markdown>
    "**Heads-up: the report will be a day late**, Thursday instead of Wednesday. The data export took
    longer than expected. Everything else is on track. **Is Thursday morning okay**, or should I send a
    partial version on Wednesday?"
    </div>

    Tell people **as soon as you know**, not on the deadline day.

## Meetings: take back your calendar

- Decline politely: "**Thanks for the invite.** I don't think I'm needed for this one. Could you share the notes?"
- Ask for an agenda: "**Could you share an agenda** so I can prepare?"
- Shorten: "**Could we do this in 15 minutes** instead of 30?"
- Async: "**Could this be a Slack thread** instead of a meeting?"

## Conversation: too many priorities

```dialogue
You: Do you have ten minutes? I need help prioritising this week.
Manager: Sure. What's going on?
You: I've got the search release on Friday, the client bug, three interviews, and the architecture doc.
Manager: That's a lot. What's at risk?
You: If I do everything, the release slips to Monday and the doc doesn't happen.
Manager: The release and the client bug are the top two. Can someone else take one interview?
You: Ravi offered to. And the architecture doc could move to next week.
Manager: Agreed. Release first, then the bug, two interviews, and the doc next week.
You: Thanks. I'll update the interview panel and block focus time for the release.
```

## Practice

one message you need to send to renegotiate something.
