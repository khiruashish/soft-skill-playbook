# Improving Soft Skills: The 90-Day Plan

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**What gets measured gets improved.** Score yourself, pick your track, and spend 10–20 minutes a day on it.
</div>

## Step 1: Self-assessment

Score each from **1 (rarely)** to **5 (always)**. Redo it every 30 days.

| # | Skill | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I state my main point in the first sentence | ☐ |
| 2 | I speak up in meetings without being asked | ☐ |
| 3 | I can explain a technical issue to a non-technical person | ☐ |
| 4 | I raise risks early, before they become problems | ☐ |
| 5 | I say no or push back politely when needed | ☐ |
| 6 | I give feedback that's specific and kind | ☐ |
| 7 | I listen without interrupting | ☐ |
| 8 | I stay calm when someone is upset or criticises me | ☐ |
| 9 | I can make small talk with a stranger for 3+ minutes | ☐ |
| 10 | I write clear, short messages and emails | ☐ |

**10–25:** Beginner track · **26–40:** Intermediate track · **41–50:** Advanced track

## Step 2: Choose your track

=== ":material-sprout: Beginner track"

    **Month 1: Foundations**

    - [ ] Read [Communication Principles](communication-principles.md) and [Building Confidence](building-confidence.md)
    - [ ] Speak at least once in every meeting
    - [ ] Use the [stand-up format](../workplace/day-to-day.md) every day
    - [ ] One 10-minute [Master Prompt](../master-prompt.md) role-play a day
    - [ ] Learn 5 phrases a week from the [Phrase Bank](../reference/phrase-bank.md)

    **Month 2: Workplace**

    - [ ] Prepare an agenda for every 1:1 with your manager
    - [ ] Write one bug report using [SBAR](../workplace/explaining-issues.md)
    - [ ] Ask one clarifying question on every new task
    - [ ] Have a 5-minute conversation with someone outside your team each week

    **Month 3: Visibility**

    - [ ] Give a 5-minute demo or knowledge-share
    - [ ] Practise one [Everyday Life](../course/phase-3.md) scenario in real life each week
    - [ ] Ask your manager: *"What's one thing about how I communicate that I could improve?"*

=== ":material-trending-up: Intermediate track"

    **Month 1: Influence**

    - [ ] Use PREP in every design discussion
    - [ ] Push back on one unrealistic estimate using options, not "no"
    - [ ] Mentor one junior with a weekly 30-minute session

    **Month 2: Up-level your audience**

    - [ ] Write a one-page decision doc for a non-technical stakeholder
    - [ ] Present at a team-wide or cross-team meeting
    - [ ] Practise the [CEO 30-second update](../workplace/talking-to-ceo.md) with AI 5 times

    **Month 3: Hard conversations**

    - [ ] Have one [difficult conversation](../workplace/difficult-conversations.md) you've been avoiding
    - [ ] Run a blameless post-mortem
    - [ ] Get 360° feedback from a peer, a junior and your manager

=== ":material-crown: Advanced track (8–15+ yrs)"

    **Month 1: Executive presence**

    - [ ] Shorten every update by 30%; lead with the decision
    - [ ] Pre-wire important meetings (talk to key people 1:1 beforehand)
    - [ ] Turn one technical proposal into a business case (cost, risk, revenue)

    **Month 2: Multiply others**

    - [ ] Coach two people on communication, using this playbook
    - [ ] Delegate one high-visibility task and sponsor the person publicly
    - [ ] Facilitate (don't dominate) a design review: speak last

    **Month 3: External voice**

    - [ ] Give a talk at a meetup or internal tech conference
    - [ ] Write a blog post or internal article
    - [ ] Practise the networking scripts at one event

## Step 3: Daily 10-minute drills

| Drill | How | Builds |
|---|---|---|
| **The 60-second explain** | Pick a concept (Kafka, caching, OAuth). Explain it aloud in 60 s to an imaginary 12-year-old. | Clarity |
| **Rewrite one message** | Take a Slack message you sent. Make it 30% shorter with the point first. | Brevity |
| **Shadowing** | Play a 1-minute clip of a good speaker (TED, a podcast). Repeat each sentence right after them. | Fluency, pace |
| **Record & review** | Record a mock stand-up on your phone. Watch it once at 1x. | Voice, fillers |
| **AI role-play** | Run the [Master Prompt](../master-prompt.md) on today's real situation. | Everything |
| **Word of the day** | Learn one professional phrase and use it in a real conversation. | Vocabulary |
| **Reflection journal** | 3 lines: *What I said · How it landed · What I'll try next.* | Self-awareness |

## Step 4: Measure progress

- Redo the self-assessment every 30 days.
- Count: how many times did you speak up this week? How many messages did you rewrite before sending?
- Ask for feedback each quarter: *"On a scale of 1–10, how clear are my updates? What would make them a 10?"*

## Practice conversation: Asking for feedback on your communication

```dialogue
You: Could I ask for some feedback? I'm working on my communication this quarter.
Manager: Of course.
You: On a scale of 1 to 10, how clear are my updates in stand-ups?
Manager: Maybe a 6. They're accurate, but sometimes too detailed.
You: That's helpful. What would make them a 9?
Manager: Start with the result, then only the important detail. Keep it under a minute.
You: Okay. I'll try that for the next two weeks. Could we check again at the end of the month?
Manager: Sure, let's do that.
```
