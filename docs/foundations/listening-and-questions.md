# Listening & Asking Questions

<div class="ssp-banner" markdown>
**Good listeners ask good questions, and people trust them more.** Listening is the most underrated skill
on this site.
</div>

## Active listening in 4 moves

<div class="ssp-stack">
<div>1. Pay attention <span>— close the laptop, look at them, don't plan your reply</span></div>
<div>2. Acknowledge <span>— "Okay", "I see", a nod; show you're with them</span></div>
<div>3. Paraphrase <span>— "So what you're saying is…"</span></div>
<div>4. Clarify <span>— "When you say 'urgent', do you mean today or this week?"</span></div>
</div>

!!! example "Paraphrase in practice"
    **Manager:** "The client is unhappy, the dashboard keeps breaking and nobody tells them anything."

    **You:** "So there are two problems: the dashboard's reliability, and how we communicate when it
    breaks. Which one hurts them more right now?"

## Question types and when to use them

| Type | Example | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | "How do you see this working?" | Exploring, building rapport |
| **Closed** | "Is the deadline Friday?" | Confirming a fact |
| **Clarifying** | "What does 'done' mean for this ticket?" | Requirements are vague |
| **Probing** | "What makes you say that?" | Getting to the real reason |
| **Hypothetical** | "What if we shipped without the export feature?" | Negotiating scope |
| **Reflective** | "It sounds like you're frustrated with the delays?" | Emotions are high |
| **Summary** | "So we agreed X, Y, Z — right?" | Closing a conversation |

## Questions every developer should ask

=== "Before starting a task"

    - What problem are we solving, and for whom?
    - What does **done** look like? How will we test it?
    - What is the deadline, and is it fixed or flexible?
    - Who else is affected? Who should review it?
    - Is there anything you've already tried or decided?

=== "When you're stuck"

    - "I've tried A and B. Before I go further, am I on the right track?"
    - "Who knows the most about this module?"
    - "Can you point me to an example of how we usually do this?"

=== "In a 1:1"

    - "What's the most important thing I should focus on this quarter?"
    - "What's one thing I could do better?"
    - "What does the next level look like for me?"

=== "With a stakeholder"

    - "What would success look like for you?"
    - "If we can only deliver one of these, which matters most?"
    - "What happens if this is a week late?"

## How to ask a "stupid" question without feeling stupid

Use the **context + attempt + question** pattern:

<div class="ssp-say" markdown>
"I'm new to the payments module **(context)**. I read the README and traced the `refund()` call
**(attempt)**, but I can't see where the ledger entry is created. **Can you point me in the right
direction? (question)**"
</div>

It shows you put in effort first, and people respect that.

## Listening blockers to avoid

- [ ] Interrupting to show you already know the answer
- [ ] Solving before the person has finished describing the problem
- [ ] Checking your phone or Slack mid-conversation
- [ ] "That's nothing, once I had…" (turning their story into yours)
- [ ] Filling every silence; give people 3 seconds to think
