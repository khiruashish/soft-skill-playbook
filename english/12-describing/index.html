    Describing People, Places & Problems¶

    Today's goal: describe people, places, things and problems clearly, and compare options. You'll use this to report lost items, explain a bug, choose a hotel, or find a person in a crowd.

    Adjectives: where they go¶

    a / an + adjective + noun · be + adjective
    • a big black suitcase · an old building · a quiet room
    • The room is quiet. · The app is slow. · She looks tired.

    Order (when you use several): opinion → size → age → colour → material → noun: "a nice small old red leather bag" (in practice, use 2–3).

    Describing people¶

    Topic Words
    Height & build tall, short, average height, slim, well-built
    Hair short / long, straight / curly, black / grey, bald
    Age young, middle-aged, elderly, in his thirties
    Clothes wearing a blue shirt, a red saree, glasses, a cap
    Personality friendly, helpful, quiet, confident, patient, strict

    "He's about 60, short, with grey hair and glasses. He's wearing a white kurta."

    Describing places and things¶

    • Place: crowded, quiet, clean, noisy, modern, spacious, well-located, far from / close to…
    • Things: heavy, light, broken, new, second-hand, waterproof, made of steel / plastic / cotton
    • Location words: next to, opposite, behind, in front of, between, near, on the corner of

    Comparing¶

    Short adjectives Long adjectives Irregular
    cheap → cheaper → the cheapest expensive → more expensive → the most expensive good → better → the best
    big → bigger → the biggest comfortable → more comfortable bad → worse → the worst
    easy → easier → the easiest convenient → more convenient far → farther → the farthest
    • The metro is faster than the bus. · This is the cheapest option. · It's not as big as the other room.

    Describing a problem (any kind)¶

    What's wrong → since when → how bad → what you tried
    Situation Description
    Lost bag "It's a medium-sized black backpack with a red keychain. It has my laptop inside."
    Broken appliance "The washing machine makes a loud noise and doesn't drain. It started yesterday."
    App bug "The app freezes on the payment screen every time I tap 'Pay'. I've restarted the phone."
    Health "I've had a sharp pain in my lower back for three days. It's worse in the morning."

    Useful problem words: broken, not working, stuck, leaking, cracked, missing, damaged, too loud, too slow, freezing, overheating, keeps (+ -ing): "It keeps crashing."

    Conversation: lost bag at the metro station¶

    YouExcuse me, I think I left my bag on the train.
    StaffWhich train, and what time?
    YouThe one that arrived at platform 2 at about 9:15, from Whitefield.
    StaffCan you describe the bag?
    YouIt's a medium-sized black backpack. It's quite heavy, and it has a red keychain on the zip.
    StaffWhat's inside?
    YouMy laptop, a charger, and a blue water bottle. My office ID card is in the front pocket.
    StaffOkay. Please fill in this form. We'll check with the last station.
    YouThank you. How long does it usually take?
    StaffAbout an hour. Please wait near the customer care counter.
    YouOkay. Here's my phone number, in case you find it.

    Practice¶

    1. Compare: cab vs metro (fast, cheap, comfortable).

    Sample answer

    The metro is cheaper and faster than a cab during rush hour, but a cab is more comfortable.

    2. Describe a person you can see right now in 3 sentences (height, hair, clothes).

    3. Describe a problem with something at home using: what's wrong → since when → how bad → what you tried.

    Sample answer

    The kitchen tap is leaking. It started two days ago. It drips all the time, and there's water on the floor. I tightened it, but it didn't help.

    Speak today¶

    • Describe your home or office to someone in 5 sentences
    • Compare two options using -er than / more … than
    • Describe one problem clearly (at home, at work, or with an app)
