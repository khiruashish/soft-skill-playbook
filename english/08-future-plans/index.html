    Future: Plans & Promises¶

    Today's goal: talk about plans, predictions, promises and arrangements, and ask people about theirs.

    Three ways to talk about the future¶

    Form Use for Example
    will + verb decisions now, promises, offers, predictions I'll call you back. · It will rain later.
    going to + verb plans you already decided; clear evidence I'm going to buy a new laptop. · Look at those clouds, it's going to rain.
    present continuous fixed arrangements (time/place agreed) I'm meeting the client at 3 tomorrow. · We're flying to Delhi on Friday.

    will¶

    Positive Negative Question
    I'll send it today. I won't (will not) be late. Will you be there?
    It'll be ready by 5. It won't take long. When will it be ready?

    Promises & offers: "I'll fix it tonight." · "I'll help you with that." · "Shall I book a cab?" (Shall I / we = offer or suggestion.)

    going to¶

    Positive Negative Question
    We're going to move to Pune. I'm not going to take the job. Are you going to apply?

    Talking about plans & time¶

    • this evening / weekend · next week / month / year · in two days / an hour
    • tomorrow morning · the day after tomorrow · by Friday (= not later than Friday)
    • until 6 (= continuing up to 6) · soon · later today

    Questions about plans¶

    • What are you doing this weekend?
    • Are you going to take any leave this year?
    • When will you know the result?
    • Will you be free after lunch?
    • What time are you leaving tomorrow?

    Conversation: planning a weekend trip¶

    FriendAre you doing anything this weekend?
    YouYes! We're going to Coorg on Saturday morning.
    FriendNice! How are you getting there?
    YouWe're driving. We're going to leave at six to avoid traffic.
    FriendWhere are you staying?
    YouWe've booked a homestay near Madikeri. We're coming back on Sunday evening.
    FriendSounds lovely. Will it be cold?
    YouProbably. I'll pack some jackets.
    FriendSend me photos!
    YouI will. I'll message you when we get there.

    Practice¶

    1. will, going to, or present continuous?

    1. A: "The phone is ringing." B: "I ______ (answer) it."
    2. We ______ (visit) my parents next Sunday. It's all planned.
    3. I promise I ______ (not tell) anyone.
    4. I've decided: I ______ (learn) Kubernetes this year.
    Answers
    1. 'll answer 2. are visiting (or are going to visit) 3. won't tell 4. 'm going to learn

    2. Make questions. (you / do / tonight / what) · (the results / be / ready / when) · (you / apply / for the job)

    Answers

    What are you doing tonight? · When will the results be ready? · Are you going to apply for the job?

    3. Write 3 plans for next week: one with going to, one arrangement with time, one promise with will.

    Speak today¶

    • Tell someone your plans for this weekend
    • Make one promise at work with a time: "I'll send it by 4 pm."
    • Offer help with "Shall I…?"
