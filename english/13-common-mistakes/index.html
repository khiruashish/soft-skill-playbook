    Common Mistakes & Fixes¶

    Today's goal: fix the mistakes that many Indian English speakers make. You'll recognise most of them. Fixing just these makes your English sound much clearer and more natural.

    Articles: a, an, the¶

    Rule ✅ Example
    a before a consonant sound a bank, a user, a university (sounds like "you")
    an before a vowel sound an app, an hour (silent h), an MBA ("em")
    the when both people know which one Please close the door. · The meeting is at 3.
    No article for general plurals / uncountables Developers like coffee. · I need information.
    Places of routine (no "the") go to work / school / bed / college
    "the" for unique things the sun, the internet, the CEO

    ❌ I am going to office. → ✅ I'm going to the office. (or "going to work")

    Prepositions¶

    ❌ Common mistake ✅ Correct
    discuss about the plan discuss the plan (no "about")
    reach to office reach the office / get to the office
    married with her married to her
    good in English good at English
    angry on me angry with me
    wait for 5 minutes ✔ / wait me wait for me
    on the weekend / at the weekend ✔ on Monday, in May, at 6 pm
    He is in leave He's on leave
    I live here since 5 years I've lived here for 5 years / since 2021

    Tenses & verbs¶

    ❌ Mistake ✅ Fix Rule
    I am having two kids. I have two kids. "have" for owning isn't -ing
    I am knowing him. I know him. state verbs: know, understand, want, like
    I have seen him yesterday. I saw him yesterday. finished time → past simple
    He don't know. He doesn't know. he/she/it → doesn't
    Did you went? Did you go? did + base verb
    I am working here since 2020. I've been working here since 2020. since/for → present perfect
    Myself Ashish. I'm Ashish. / My name is Ashish. "myself" isn't used to introduce

    Indian English that confuses global listeners¶

    Instead of Say
    Do the needful Could you please [specific action]?
    I have a doubt I have a question
    Revert back Reply / Get back to me
    Prepone Move earlier / Bring forward
    Out of station Travelling / Out of town
    Today itself / Now itself Today / Right now
    Pass out (from college) Graduate
    Updation Update
    Kindly do it fast Could you please do it soon?
    Only (at the end: "I told you only") I told you. / I told you already.
    Is it? (as reaction) Really? / Oh, is that right?

    Pronunciation hotspots¶

    • v and w: very (teeth on lip) vs west (round lips)
    • th: think (tongue between teeth), not "tink"
    • Stress: DEVeloper, deVELop, COMpany, ANalyst, hoTEL, CALendar
    • Don't add a vowel before s + consonant: "school" not "is-school", "station" not "is-station"

    Conversation: a quick self-check¶

    ColleagueDid you discuss the release plan with the client?
    YouYes, I discussed the plan with them this morning.
    ColleagueAnd when is the release?
    YouThey want to bring it forward by a week, to the 10th.
    ColleagueOh! Is Ravi available? I heard he's on leave.
    YouHe's on leave until Wednesday, but he'll reply to emails.
    ColleagueOkay. I have a question about the test environment, actually.
    YouSure. I've been working on it since Monday, so I can help.

    Practice¶

    Fix all the mistakes.

    1. Myself Priya, I am working in TCS since 4 years.
    2. Please do the needful and revert back today itself.
    3. I have a doubt, we can discuss about it tomorrow?
    4. He is in leave, he will reach to office on Monday.
    5. I am having an Honda bike.
    Answers
    1. I'm Priya. I've been working at TCS for four years.
    2. Could you please approve it and reply today?
    3. I have a question. Can we discuss it tomorrow?
    4. He's on leave. He'll get to the office on Monday.
    5. I have a Honda bike. (H in "Honda" is pronounced, so "a")

    Speak today¶

    • Choose 3 mistakes from this page that you make, and write them on a sticky note
    • Catch yourself once today and correct it out loud
    • Ask the AI tutor: "Track my mistakes from this list in every lesson"
